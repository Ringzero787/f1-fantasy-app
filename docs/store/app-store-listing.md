## Description

```
Undercut is a fantasy motorsport game played over a Formula 1 season.

Pick five drivers and a constructor inside a fixed budget, then manage that team
weekend to weekend. Prices move with form, contracts run out, and the bank you
have on Sunday is the one your choices left you.

Your lineup locks when the weekend's first scoring session begins. One pick can
be named your Ace for double points, and the Ace stays yours to move after
qualifying, right up to the start of the race.

Create a private league and invite friends by code, or play solo against the
standings. Points come from qualifying, the sprint where there is one, and the
race.

Unofficial. Not affiliated with Formula 1, the FIA, or any team.
```
