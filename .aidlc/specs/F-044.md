# F-044 — UC: Auto-fill by value at lock time

Created: 2026-09-17 · Status: planned

## Goal

Undercut players whose driver or constructor contracts expire currently get the seat re-bought immediately by the cheapest car on the grid. By round 16 of 2026, 37 of 40 live teams were entirely auto-filled, holding $200–550 rosters with $700–1,400 idle in the bank. This change (a) leaves seats open at expiry so the owner can choose, with the existing incomplete-team reminders nudging them, and (b) fills whatever is still empty when the weekend locks with the best-value combination the bank affords, ranked by fantasy points per race over the last five scored rounds. Server only, plus the Rules Guide copy.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `onRaceCompleted` Phase 3.5 still sells expired drivers/constructors at market price, banks their points into `lockedPoints`, decrements `totalPoints`, and records the 1-race lockout — but no longer adds replacement drivers or a replacement constructor. Budget = bank + sale returns.
- [ ] `autoLockTeams` fills incomplete teams (fewer than 5 drivers or no constructor) at the moment it locks them, using `planAutoFill`: seats filled first, then highest recent form, then lowest cost, within `team.budget`.
- [ ] Locked-out drivers (`driverLockouts[id] > completedRaceCount`) and drivers already on the team are never auto-filled.
- [ ] A team that has never fielded a car and has never been scored is not auto-filled.
- [ ] Auto-filled entries carry `isReservePick: true`, `racesHeld: 0`, `contractLength: 3`, `addedAtRace = completedRaceCount`; the team gets `budget` reduced by the cost, `totalSpent` incremented, and a `lastAutoFill` stamp (`raceId`, `driverIds`, `constructorId`, `cost`, `at`).
- [ ] Market and form are loaded at most once per `autoLockTeams` run, and only when at least one team is incomplete.
- [ ] `node --test functions/test/autoFill.test.js` passes on the compiled output and is part of the repo test gate.
- [ ] The manual repair path (`repairTeamScoring`) mirrors expiry without auto-fill.
- [ ] Rules Guide "Auto-Fill" section describes the new behaviour (seat stays open; filled at lock by value; reserve picks sell fee-free).

## Targets
- android (Rules Guide copy; behaviour change is server-side)
- functions (Undercut codebase `default`: `autoLockTeams`, `onRaceCompleted`, `repairTeamScoring`)

## Data
No new user data. Writes `lastAutoFill` (race id, entity ids, cost, timestamp) on the player's own `fantasyTeams` doc.

## Performance budget
`autoLockTeams`: +3 collection reads (completed races, active drivers, active constructors) and one `raceScores` `in` query of ≤5 race ids, once per run, only when a team is incomplete. `onRaceCompleted` Phase 3.5 does strictly less work than before.

## Out of scope
- Client changes to how empty seats render (already supported: new teams start empty).
- A "we filled X for you" notification (the `lastAutoFill` stamp is there for it).
- Changing contract defaults or the loyalty ladder.
