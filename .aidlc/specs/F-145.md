# F-145 — UC: Pit Wall strategies (stars and scrubs, balanced, value) read from the first picks

Created: 2026-11-13 · Status: building · App: Undercut (app only, next store build) · Stacked on F-144

## Goal

The owner wants Pit Wall to have a few preset strategies, chosen by which drivers the player taps first. They approved three on 2026-11-13:
- **Stars & scrubs:** the strongest driver the bank allows, with the rest as cheap as works.
- **Balanced:** the most projected points with every seat in a sensible price band.
- **Value:** the most projected points per dollar.

Before this, the picker marked one PIT WALL PICK row and one BEST VALUE row, with no plan for the rest of the lineup.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Reading the strategy.** It comes from the drivers added this session (pending, not already on the roster). The tier is measured against the whole driver market, in thirds by price:
  - any top-third pick → STARS
  - only bottom-third picks → VALUE
  - anything else → BALANCED
  - nothing added yet → no strategy, and the picker keeps today's marks
- [ ] **The player can switch.** A PIT WALL STRATEGY strip under the tabs (drivers tab, unlocked, open seats, projections loaded) shows the three as a segment control. It says "READ FROM YOUR PICKS" until the player chooses, then the player's choice sticks.
- [ ] **The plan.** It fills exactly the open driver seats from drivers that are not picked, not locked out and have a positive projection. It stays within the bank after the cheapest constructor, when the lineup still needs one. Those rows carry PIT WALL PLAN, with the gold band, in place of the pick and best-value marks. The strip shows the plan's cost and projected points, or "NO PLAN FITS THE BANK".
- [ ] **The rules.**
  - STARS maximises the single best projection, then the total.
  - BALANCED maximises the total with every seat between 0.5× and 1.5× the even share of the bank. When no such set exists, it falls back to the most points.
  - VALUE maximises points per dollar.
  - The search is exhaustive (at most 4 open seats once a driver is picked).
- [ ] **Analytics:** `pitwall_strategy_shown` with `strategy` and `chosen`, once per strategy and source.
- [ ] **Tests:** `__tests__/pitwall/strategies.test.ts` covers detection, each strategy's plan, the bank, missing projections, the balanced fallback and null cases. Typecheck and the full jest suite pass.

## Targets

## Out of scope
- **Constructor strategy:** the plan covers drivers only. The constructor is held back at its cheapest price, as the picker's reserve already does.
- **Planning a whole lineup from nothing:** a strategy starts with the first driver tapped.
- **Ace placement within a strategy:** F-143 handles the ace after the save.
