# F-125 — UC: Market reachable while locked

Created: 2026-10-25 · Status: implemented, unreleased · Reported from the field by the owner,
2026-10-25: "the market should be accessible whenever. you can only get there via edit. so when
lock down is set you cannot. you can put it on the same line where team 1 and team 2 are just
locked to the right position if that works."

## Goal
Undercut (app only; no server, rules or portal change). The picker is the only place a player can
see every driver's price, points, value and Pit Wall projection, and until now it opened from the
Team screen's EDIT → alone, which the lock disables. So from qualifying until the race was scored
nobody could look at the market at all — the time a player most wants to study it. A MARKET →
entry now sits on the team-switcher row, right-aligned as the owner asked, and stays live through
the lock. Opened while locked, the picker is a market to read: nothing can be added or removed, and
the screen says so once in the title, the status strip and the save bar instead of dimming forty
rows.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **A MARKET → entry on the team-switcher row, right-aligned**, under the season progress bar,
      with the TEAM 1 / TEAM 2 / + NEW TEAM controls on its left when they exist and nothing else
      on the row when they do not (the row used to render only with a second team in play; it now
      always renders for this entry). The row wraps like the LINEUP row below it, so at display
      size XXL with a large OS font MARKET → drops to its own line rather than off the edge (not
      checked on a device). Hidden while the second-team create form is open, since the form
      replaces the rest of the screen. (`GridTeamPanel`;
      `docs/design/grid/verify/F-125-web-team-market-entry-unlocked.png`.)
- [x] **It opens the picker whether or not the lineup is locked.** The press handler has no lock
      gate, unlike EDIT → (which keeps its LOCKED state: it is the action to change the lineup, and
      that is still refused). The accessibility hint says which of the two the player is getting.
      (`F-125-F-126-web-team-locked-market-entry-counter-moved.png`.)
- [x] **Locked, the picker reads as a market, not a broken picker.** Title MARKET instead of
      LOCKED, ← BACK instead of ← CANCEL, every row at full strength with its number, name, team,
      points, value, projection, trend and price — rows are no longer dimmed to 35% for the lock,
      only for the picker's own reasons (full roster, locked out, cannot afford) — and the save bar
      says LOCKED · AUTO-FILL ON as before. Sorting and the DRIVERS / CONSTRUCTOR tabs work.
      (`F-125-web-market-locked-drivers.png`, `F-125-web-market-locked-constructors.png`.)
- [x] **No row responds to a tap while locked.** The press handler returns before touching the
      pending lineup, the row is disabled, and a screenshot before and after a forced row tap is
      pixel-identical (Playwright, demo mode with the clock set four hours after qualifying).
- [x] **The Moonshot rockets follow the race's lock time**, the one the server checks for a call
      (`effectiveLockTime`, not the team's own modify flag): after it a new call cannot be made,
      so only this race's existing call keeps its rocket on its driver's row, to look at it. Before
      it every driver row has one as before. A device clock behind the server's shows rockets for
      a few minutes longer; the sheet then shows the server's refusal, as on the Team screen.
- [x] **Nothing changed for the unlocked picker** beyond the title and the back label being the
      same as before: `blocked` no longer carries the lock, and the lock is read where the rows are
      rendered and pressed instead. Typecheck, lint and the existing tests pass.

## Targets
- android
- ios

## Data
No user data. The picker reads what it always read; nothing new is stored or sent.

## Performance budget
One more Pressable on the Team screen. Bundle +1 KB source.

## Out of scope
- A read-only driver detail from a market row while locked (the picker's row tap opens the add
  sheet when unlocked, and adding is what the lock forbids). The Team screen's tile sheet still
  gives the stats for the roster.
- The legacy `(tabs)` UI.
- Verified on Expo web in demo mode (both themes) with the browser clock set before and after the
  demo round's qualifying; not driven on a device build. The row layout is the same primitives the
  LINEUP row uses, so the display-size XXL check from F-108 applies, but it was not repeated.
- Shipping: the next store build after 2.6.0 (`.aidlc/releases/`).
