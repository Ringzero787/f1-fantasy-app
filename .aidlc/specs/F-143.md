# F-143 — UC: Pit Wall ace call right after confirming a lineup

Created: 2026-11-13 · Status: building · App: Undercut (app only, next store build) · Stacked on F-142

## Goal

The owner wants a quick Pit Wall check the moment a lineup is confirmed: "Pit Wall should recommend an ace position after I confirm my team."

The advice already exists:
- F-119's `aceAdvice` produces it.
- The Team screen shows it as one line.
- The tile sheet leads with the call and a MAKE ACE button.

The gap is that the player has to go looking for it. Now a successful save in the picker asks the Team screen to open the tile sheet on the driver Pit Wall would ace, once.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A save that applied in full requests the ace call (`useAceCallPrompt.request()`). A partly applied save shows its alert instead and requests nothing. The Team screen answers only once it is focused again (it stays mounted under the picker), and only for a driver its roster holds.
- [ ] The Team screen answers once. When the advice is to set or move the ace, it opens the tile sheet on that driver; the sheet's own PIT WALL · ACE CALL block and MAKE ACE button do the rest. A `keep` (the ace is already right) opens nothing.
- [ ] No advice yet (projections loading, last round's projections, ace locked) leaves the request waiting. It lapses after 30 s, so a later visit never pops a stale sheet.
- [ ] Analytics: `pitwall_ace_call_after_save` with `kind`.
- [ ] Tested: `__tests__/simple/aceCallPrompt.test.ts` covers once-only, the lapse, and which advice opens. Typecheck and the full jest suite pass.

## Targets
Undercut app: `src/simple/grid/aceCallPrompt.ts`, `GridPickerScreen.tsx`, `GridTeamPanel.tsx`. Next store build.

## Data
None. In-memory only.

## Out of scope
- **Constructor ace advice:** the advice covers drivers only, as F-119 does.
- **Strategy presets** that pick a plan from the first drivers tapped: these need the owner's design.
