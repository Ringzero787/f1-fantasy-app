# F-059 — UC: Server-owned league counts, team-name check and scoped team reads

Created: 2026-09-18 · Status: planned

## Goal

Phase two of F-056. F-056 closed every league-rules hole that could be closed without breaking released clients; three remain because released builds depend on the loose behaviour. This feature removes those dependencies first (server + client), then tightens the rules once the fixed client is the minimum supported version (`config/app.minVersion`, enforced by `AppUpdateGate`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Callable `checkTeamNameAvailable({ name, excludeTeamId? })` (Undercut functions) answers the uniqueness question server-side; `teamService.createTeam` and `updateTeamName` use it instead of the global `where('name','==',…)` query. Ships in a client release.
- [ ] A Firestore trigger on `leagues/{id}/members/{uid}` create/delete (approved members only) maintains `memberCount`; the client stops writing it. Rules then deny every non-owner league update.
- [ ] After `config/app.minVersion` is at or above that client release: `fantasyTeams` `allow list` becomes `resource.data.userId == request.auth.uid || isLeagueMember(resource.data.leagueId) || isAdmin()`, verified in `rules-tests/` against the own-teams, league-teams and member-view queries.
- [ ] `maxMembers` increases only through a callable that verifies the expansion purchase; the owner's client can no longer write it.
- [ ] Each rules change deploys through `aidlc op rules-deploy` with the rules tests green.

## Targets
- firebase (functions, rules)
- android
- ios

## Data
No new user data.

## Performance budget
One callable round-trip on team create/rename (replaces one query); one trigger invocation per join/leave.

## Out of scope
- Restricting who can read league documents and invite codes (needs a join-by-code callable; separate decision).
