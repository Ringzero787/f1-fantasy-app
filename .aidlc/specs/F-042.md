# F-042 — TL: Ben lines calibration backtest report

Created: 2026-09-11 · Status: building

## Goal
Nothing measured whether Ben's lines are any good. The invariant checker (F-039) says a line is well-formed, not whether the chances it states come true, and the book's P&L was never tallied. `newgame/functions/scripts/calibrateBenLines.js` grades every settled line with settlement's own functions and reports: landed vs stated chance by probability bucket, Brier score and skill against the base rate, what backing every WITH or every AGAINST at the offered odds returned, splits by source, session, zone and race, how often DNFs caused race misses, Ben's best-bet record, the book's result from settlement records (staked calls, free-call bonuses, penalties), and whether any settled outcome no longer matches the current lines and results. It runs monthly as an advisory G15 lookback check, so Ben and the owner see mispricing in numbers rather than anecdotes.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Unit tests on a synthetic season: grading uses settlement's compiled `buildSessionResults` / `decideOutcome`; backfill_actuals sessions, legacy single-number lines, best bets and races that are not completed are left out of the model score; a worked example gives Brier 0.2075 and skill 0.17; realised returns for WITH and AGAINST; best-bet returns including the ×1.5 AGAINST boost; the book split into stakes, paid on stakes, free-call bonuses and penalties; a settled outcome that no longer matches is reported; a bucket finding needs at least 20 lines and a 15-point gap.
- [ ] The real 2026 run grades the settled races that have real ranges (R7–R15), leaves out the R1–R6 placeholder sessions, and reports whether every settled outcome matches the current lines and results.
- [ ] On the build box, `aidlc run --tier lookback --gates G15` writes the report to `/mnt/smb/f1-app/aidlc-ops/reports/calibration-latest.md` and reports its findings without blocking.
- [ ] Without Firestore credentials, or without the functions' dependencies installed (CI's lookback), `--json` prints no findings and exits 0.
- [ ] The report is never written into the repository.

## Targets
- backend tooling: a Track Limits operator script and the AIDLC lookback tier (`checks.domain`, non-blocking). No app or Cloud Function change.

## Data
Reads `races`, `ben_lines`, `constructors` and `tl_picks` (settled outcomes: stake, payout and penalty per call). The report holds aggregates only — no user ids or names. It is written only to the private SMB share, because it shows where players beat the book and this repo is public.

## Performance budget
n/a — a few seconds; reads about a thousand documents once a month.

## Out of scope
- Changing the model or the pricing (zone σ, DNF risk in race lines, best-bet odds) — decisions for Ben and the owner, informed by this report.
- Per-player analysis, and any app-facing view of these numbers.
