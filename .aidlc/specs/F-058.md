# F-058 — UC: Team tile detail sheet with stats and remove

Created: 2026-09-18 · Status: planned

## Goal

On the Grid Team screen a tile only showed a name, points and a trend, and dropping a driver meant going through Pick Team. Players asked to tap a driver (or the constructor) on the main page, see how that pick is doing, and remove it right there. Tapping a filled tile now opens a bottom sheet with the pick's stats and two actions, Ace and Remove, using the same server-authoritative store actions and the same sale quote as Pick Team. In another player's team view the sheet opens with stats only.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Tapping a filled tile on the Team screen opens `GridTileSheet`; open slots still route to Pick Team; the ACE pill on the tile keeps working.
- [ ] The sheet shows: `DRIVER · <number>` or `TEAM`, full name, team colour bar and name, `ACE 2×` / `AUTO-FILLED` markers; stat cards FOR YOUR TEAM (points scored for this team, per race held), LAST RACE (total with ▲/▼/• vs the race before), PRICE (live market price, delta vs paid); CONTRACT dots with `n OF m RACES LEFT` (red at 1); LAST RACE BREAKDOWN (finish, grid, race, quali, sprint, positions gained, fastest lap, when present); RECENT FORM as bars for the last five scored rounds (one `raceScores` query per entity, cached in the store; a failed read shows the empty state, not a spinner).
- [ ] Ace button: `MAKE ACE · 2× POINTS` / `CLEAR ACE`; disabled with an explanation when the live price is above `PRICING_CONFIG.ACE_MAX_PRICE` or when `aceLocked`; store errors surface in an alert.
- [ ] Remove: `REMOVE FROM TEAM` reveals the quote (sale price, early-termination fee or NO FEE, you receive) from `estimateSaleQuote` on the live price, then `CONFIRM · REMOVE <NAME>` calls `removeDriver` / `removeConstructor`; disabled with `LOCKED · NO CHANGES THIS WEEKEND` when the lineup is locked; store errors surface in an alert.
- [ ] Ace visibility on the Team screen: while the team has a pick but no Ace and the Ace is not locked, the ACE pills (shown only on picks whose live price is at or under `PRICING_CONFIG.ACE_MAX_PRICE`, plus the current Ace so it can be cleared) are red and a red-outlined `NO ACE SET · TAP A TILE TO PICK YOUR 2× SCORER` call-out sits under the grid; once set, a line shows `ACE <NAME> · 2× POINTS`; the `TAP A TILE FOR STATS, ACE OR REMOVE` hint is 13 px.
- [ ] A failed Ace or Remove keeps the sheet open and alerts the store's message (`runStoreAction`, jest-covered); the roster's constructor is read through `rosterConstructor()` (own-property check, jest-covered).
- [ ] Member team view (`GridMemberTeam`) opens the same sheet without Ace or Remove.
- [ ] `tileDetail()` in `src/simple/grid/tileDetail.ts` is pure with jest coverage: points per race, price delta, contract, trend, fee vs grace period vs reserve pick, ace eligibility, last-race breakdown incl. a retirement, five-round form ordering, no-score state.
- [ ] `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx jest --ci` pass; `npx expo export --platform android` succeeds. Screenshots in `docs/design/grid/verify/F-058-*`.

## Targets
- android
- ios

## Data
No new user data. Reads existing `raceScores` documents for the tapped entity.

## Performance budget
One `raceScores` query per entity the first time its sheet opens in a session (cached afterwards); nothing on the Team screen's initial render.

## Out of scope
- Swapping a driver from the sheet (Pick Team keeps that).
- Season-long charts or price history graphs.
