# F-100 — UC: Pit Wall edits the team name and the manager's name

Created: 2026-10-04 · Status: building

## Goal
Undercut (root app) and its Pit Wall portal. The portal shows the team's name across the top of the Briefing and the manager's name in the account, but both could only be changed in the app. From the portal a signed-in person can now rename their team and change their own display name, and the app shows both on its next load, because they are written to the same documents the app reads.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Team rename goes through a new callable `renameTeam({ teamId, name })` in `functions/src/teams/teamName.ts`: caller must own the team; the name is trimmed and 2–30 characters (`normalizeTeamName`, the app's rule); a name held by any other team is refused (`already-exists`); the write sets `name` and `updatedAt`. The portal has no direct write to `fantasyTeams`.
- [ ] Display name: the portal writes `users/{uid}.displayName` (which the app reads into its profile) and the Auth profile; trimmed, single-spaced, 2–30 characters, the same bounds as the app's Profile screen. `normalizeDisplayName` is exported from functions for the app to adopt later.
- [ ] Hero: the team name is editable in place (click or the pencil; Enter saves, Escape cancels; refusals shown as a sentence); a Manager line under it edits the display name the same way. The context bar and hero show the new names at once (team reload and account re-read).
- [ ] Unit tests: `normalizeDisplayName` bounds (functions), `normalizeName` and the error sentences (portal). `renameTeam` is exported from the functions index.
- [ ] Functions deploy through `aidlc op functions-deploy`; the portal through `pitwall-pages-deploy`. Rules unchanged (`users` already allows the owner to update `displayName`).

## Targets
- web
- firebase (functions)

## Data
Writes two fields the person already owns: their team's `name` and their own `displayName`. No new data collected.

## Performance budget
One callable round-trip per rename; no change to page loads.

## Out of scope
- Renaming a league, or another person's team.
- The app's own Profile screen (it keeps its client-side rename; a follow-up can switch it to the callable).
