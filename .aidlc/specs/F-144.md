# F-144 — UC: Pit Wall portal follows the live team so recommendations re-rank after an app sale

Created: 2026-11-13 · Status: building · App: Undercut (Pit Wall web portal, `web/pitwall`) · Stacked on F-143

## Goal

The owner sold an expensive driver (Antonelli) in the app, which left a large bank, but the Pit Wall portal kept recommending the first list. The portal read the player's team once per sign-in (and after its own saves). The bank behind every recommendation (`purse.room`, from `plan.bankAfter` / `real.team.budget`) therefore never saw a sale made in the app.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Live team:** the portal subscribes to the signed-in player's teams with `onSnapshot`, using the same owner-scoped query `loadTeams` uses (`userId == uid`, limit 3).
  - The rules already allow this read, so the rules do not change.
  - The listener is torn down on sign-out, uid change and unmount.
- [ ] **Recommendations re-rank:** after a sale made in the app, Briefing and Lineup Lab recommendations re-rank against the new bank. Tested in `web/pitwall/src/data/teamRefresh.test.ts`: after the expensive driver is sold, a driver the old bank could not afford becomes the top recommendation.
- [ ] **Prices refresh:** prices and the race count re-read when the tab becomes visible or gains focus, at most once a minute.
- [ ] **No redraw without change:** an update that changes nothing keeps the same objects, so the pages do not redraw.
- [ ] **Unsaved plans survive:** if the roster changes under an unsaved Lineup Lab plan, the player's own edits are replayed on the new roster (`rebaseLineup`), and a short message says they were kept.
  - The new roster becomes the saved baseline, so Save never re-buys a driver the app sold.
  - An added pick is dropped when all seats are already full.
  - Updates that echo the portal's own save are ignored while that save runs.
- [ ] **Tests and build:** `npm --prefix web/pitwall test` (172 tests), `tsc` and the production build pass.

## Targets
The Pit Wall portal (`web/pitwall`), deployed with `pitwall-pages-deploy`. No app build, functions or rules change.

## Data
No new fields. One owner-scoped listener on the documents the portal already reads. Firestore read cost: one listener per open portal tab.

## Rollback
