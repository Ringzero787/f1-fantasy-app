# F-159 — UC: re-runnable calendar reconcile op for a seeded season

Created: 2026-11-17 · Status: planned

## Goal

The rollover seeds the 2027 calendar once, by creating race documents that do not exist. After that there is no tool to correct it: the schedule sync follows a session move of at most 24 hours, and `functions/scripts/checkRaceCalendar.js` is pinned to 2026 and its 2026 fixture. A race added, dropped or moved by more than a day between December and March would have to be fixed by hand, and Track Limits reads the same documents.

Source: the 2026-11-17 EGM review of 2027 readiness (finding R-17); the evidence lines were re-checked against `origin/master` on that date. Dates are proposals worked back from the 2027-03-14 opener, not owner decisions, unless marked.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An op script compares the seeded season's `races` with the provider's meetings and sessions and prints a diff: added, dropped, moved, sprint changes.
- [ ] With `--apply` it writes only the differences, is safe to re-run, and never touches a race that is completed or locked.
- [ ] `checkRaceCalendar.js` takes the season as an argument and has a fixture for 2027.
- [ ] Tests cover an added race, a dropped race, a move of more than 24 hours and a sprint weekend change.
