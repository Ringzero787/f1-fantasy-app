# F-146 — UC: off-season Pit Wall Pass sells next season

Created: 2026-11-14 · Status: shipped

## Goal

A Pit Wall Pass is stamped with a season and runs to 31 January after it. Every purchase path
(Stripe checkout, Play/App Store/Amazon purchase validation) stamped the calendar year, so a buyer
in December 2026 paid $14.99 for a pass that ran out seven weeks later, before the 2027 season had
started. From 1 December the season being sold is the next one. A December buyer still has the
final round, because access runs until the expiry, not round by round. A current holder buys the
next season once their pass ends on 31 January, when the sale season is already the new one.
Found by the FY27 audit (2026-11-13).

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] `saleSeason(now)` is the calendar year from February to November and the next year in December; January is the calendar year (unit test `F-146: from December…`).
- [x] A pass bought on 2026-12-10 expires 2028-01-31 (same test).
- [x] `createCheckout` and `validatePurchase` stamp `saleSeason`, not `currentSeason`.
- [x] A purchase on 31 Dec 23:59:59Z and on 1 Jan 00:00Z both buy 2027; the compiled `createCheckout`, `startLeagueTrial` and `validatePurchase` call `saleSeason` (test `F-146: the year boundary…`).
- [x] A league trial started on 28 January gets its full seven days (it used the ending season and was cut to the 31st) (same test).
- [x] Checkout still refuses a user with any active pass, as before; the display season (`currentSeason`) is unchanged.

## Targets
- server (Cloud Functions: `pw-createCheckout`, `pw-startLeagueTrial`, `validatePurchase`; deployed OP-224). No app build: the app only sells while no pass is active, and a holder's pass ends 31 January, after which the sale season is already next season.

## Data
No new user data. The pass record (`users/{uid}.pass`) keeps its shape; only the season and expiry values change for purchases from December.

## Performance budget
n/a

## Out of scope
- Early renewal while a pass is active: neither the app nor the portal offers it, and `revokePass` removes the whole pass, so a refund of one of two stacked purchases would take both. Needs per-grant revoke first.
- Store copy saying which season a December buyer gets (Stripe's product name already says "Pit Wall Pass 2027").
- Season-aware ingestion and the 2027 season reset (separate FY27 items).
