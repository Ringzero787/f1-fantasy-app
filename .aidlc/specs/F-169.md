# F-169 — UC: contract length capped by the season and six races in a row

Created: 2026-11-23 · Status: building

## Goal
The owner could sign a driver for 6 races with 1 race left in the season, and nothing stopped a driver being held far longer than intended. There were three gaps:
- The server allowed contracts of up to 10 races (the app offered 6), with no regard to the calendar. A contract that outlasts the season also quotes an early-termination fee on races that will never run.
- Only an *expired* contract locked a driver out for a race. Selling a driver one race before expiry and re-buying them in the same window started a fresh contract, so a driver could be held for 11 races in a row.
- `buildTeamSecure` (an emptied roster rebuilt in one call) skipped the expiry lockout entirely.

Owner rule (2026-11-23): a contract always takes the season length and races left into account; at most 6 races in a row with a single driver; then that driver is locked out for 1 race.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Every purchase (`addDriverSecure`, `setConstructorSecure`, `buildTeamSecure`, auto-fill) gets contract = min(requested, 6, races left this season, 6 − races already held in a row). Races left = this season's races not completed or cancelled. With none left (season over, rollover not run), 1. (contractRules.test.js)
- [ ] The server **clamps** a too-long request, never refuses it: shipped builds send their default of 3, and with 2 races left that must still buy. A non-integer or < 1 is still refused.
- [ ] Six in a row: selling a driver records `driverStreaks[id] = { held, atRace }` (held = `streakBefore` + `racesHeld`). A re-buy before the next race completes continues the run: the new row carries `streakBefore`, and its contract is capped at 6 − held. At 6 the purchase is refused with a "locked out until after the next race" message. A race without the driver breaks the run (`atRace` no longer matches). Contract expiry keeps its existing 1-race lockout.
- [ ] `driverStreaks` is server-owned: added to the team update denylist in `firestore.rules`. The season rollover already deletes it, because it deletes every field it does not keep.
- [ ] `buildTeamSecure` honours the expiry lockout and the run, like `addDriverSecure`.
- [ ] Auto-fill stamps purchases on this season's clock (it counted every completed race ever, so from 2027 every lockout would have read as expired). It clamps the contract and skips a driver at a full run.
- [ ] The app's picker offers only lengths 1..max, starts on the default or the longest allowed, and says why when max < 6 ("2 RACES LEFT THIS SEASON", "ON YOUR TEAM 4 RACES IN A ROW · 2 MORE AT MOST"). A driver at a full run is a blocked row with its own reason. The Rules Guide says 1–6, the season cap and six in a row. (contractChoices.test.ts)

## Targets
- Undercut functions: `addDriverSecure`, `removeDriverSecure`, `setConstructorSecure`, `buildTeamSecure`, and the lock sweep (`autoLockTeams` via autoFill). Deploy via the `functions-deploy` op, which the owner applies.
- `firestore.rules` (one field added to the denylist): the `rules-deploy` op.
- App (Grid picker, Rules Guide, types): next store build. The server half protects every build already shipped.

## Data
New server-owned team field `driverStreaks` (driver id → races held and a race count). New optional roster-row field `streakBefore`. No personal data, and no existing data changes: production has no contract over 6 (556 at 3, 2 at 5, 1 at 6, 1 at 2 on 2026-11-23).

## Performance budget
A purchase reads the season's races once (about 25 documents) in place of the completed-race count query. Negligible.

## Out of scope
- Constructors have no run limit or lockout (the owner's rule names drivers; there was never a constructor lockout). They do get the season cap and the cap of 6.
- The legacy market screen (`app/(tabs)/market`) still offers 1–5; the server clamps whatever it sends.
- Whether auto-fill should count as a transfer for the stale-roster penalty (open owner decision).
