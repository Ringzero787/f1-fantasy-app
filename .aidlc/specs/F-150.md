# F-150 — UC: season rollover - archive 2026, reset teams and standings, 2027 prices and calendar

Created: 2026-11-15 · Status: implemented

## Goal

Undercut has no season concept: a team's points, budget, roster, contracts and locks, the league
tables, and every price would run straight on from 2026 into 2027, and the only seeder
(`seedData.ts`) would overwrite live prices and reset live race documents. This adds the rollover,
run once by op after the 2026 finale is scored. The owner chose (2026-11-15):

- **Teams: fresh start, same team.** Each team's 2026 season is archived at
  `fantasyTeams/{id}/seasons/2026`; the team returns to what a new team looks like (empty roster,
  budget 1000, no points, locks or contracts) and keeps its id, owner, name, league and avatar.
- **Leagues: kept, standings reset.** The final table and champion(s) are archived at
  `leagues/{id}/seasons/2026`; every member restarts at zero.
- **Prices: from 2026 points.** Points per race scored × $6 for drivers (the 2026 method's $10, scaled
  so 2027 opens at 2026's level — owner, 2026-11-15), × $3 for constructors (a constructor scores both cars; 2026 opened them priced
  like one driver), floor $5, newcomer price $5 below five scored races (2026 opened its rookies at $5).
- **Calendar from OpenF1:** the 2027 race documents (24 races, 10 sprint weekends) from OpenF1's
  2027 meetings and sessions, with circuit, city and timezone from the 2026 races, plus Sakhir,
  Algarve and Istanbul.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] `functions/src/season/rollover.ts`: `teamResetPatch` sets every new-team field to its `createTeamSecure` value and deletes every other field except userId, name, leagueId, createdAt, updatedAt, avatarUrl, avatarGeneratedAt; team creation imports the same `UNLOCKED_LOCK_STATUS` and `START_BUDGET`.
- [x] `teamArchive` keeps only server-owned fields: the season total the league ranked (active + banked + Moonshot), its three parts, best race, scored races, Moonshot stats and the final lineup. Client-writable race wins, races played and points history are left out (a planted or oversized value would read as the record, or stall the run); the league archive likewise omits the owner-writable `raceResultIds`.
- [x] `leagueArchive` keeps the final order and the champion(s): a shared top score is a shared title, a league where nobody scored has none. `memberResetPatches` zeroes totals, Moonshot points and race wins, removes the 2026 movement fields and ranks members 1..n in the scorer's tie order.
- [x] `openingPrice`: per race scored (a missed race does not lower a price), floor $5, newcomer $5 below 5 scored races, tier from the price (A > 240, B > 120). `entityPricePatch` sets price = previousPrice, moves this season's fantasyPoints to seasonPoints and zeroes fantasyPoints.
- [x] `functions/src/season/calendar.ts`: `buildCalendar` makes one race per non-cancelled meeting with a Race, numbered by race start, ids `<2026 slug>_2027`, schedule keys as the schedule sync writes them, `hasSprint` from a listed Sprint; it throws on an unknown circuit, a meeting from another season, or two meetings on one id.
- [x] `planRollover` (pure) turns the data into atomic write groups: one per team (archive create + reset), one per league (archive create + every member + league reset), all prices, the calendar (creates only), and the season switch last (`seasons/2027` active, `seasons/2026` inactive with every entity's final points, `config/app.currentSeason = '2027'`).
- [x] Re-runnable after a partial failure, and not by anything a player can write: a team or league is done when its `seasons/2026` archive exists (created in the same group as the reset; no rule lets a client write it). A player-set `seasonId: '2027'` does not skip the reset (test). An existing archive is never rewritten; a priced entity (`pricedFor`, server-only collections) is not re-priced; races are only created.
- [x] `functions/scripts/seasonRollover.js` (uc-script op): the dry run prints preconditions, the top teams, each league's champion, every old → new price with its basis and the lineup cost range, the calendar, the group count and the op's backup path list. `--apply` refuses unless at least one 2026 race is completed, none is open, the last ran ≥ 24 h ago, no 2026 result is held for review, and the season has not already been rolled; it commits one batch per group in order; a group that fails is reported and skipped so one team cannot hold everyone in 2026, and the season switch is committed only on a run where every group went in (re-run to finish).
- [x] Backup and rollback: the path list names every archive document, `seasons/2027` and every `races/*_2027` by path, so a restore deletes what the rollover created (a document recorded absent is deleted on restore).
- [x] Account deletion (`onUserDeleted`): teams are deleted recursively (their `raceSnapshots` and `seasons` archives with them — `raceSnapshots` were left behind before this), and the user's row and champion entry in every `leagues/*/seasons/*` archive lose user id and name (found by `memberUserIds`, collection-group index in firestore.indexes.json).
- [x] Calendar input is checked: a Race without a start time or a meeting name that is not a plain race name stops the build.
- [x] Dry run against production on 2026-11-15: refuses (Las Vegas, Qatar, Abu Dhabi open); 97 teams, 27 leagues, 39 members, 23 drivers, 11 constructors, 24 races from OpenF1; 127 groups, 348 writes.
- [x] Tests: functions 283/283 (`seasonRollover.test.js` +16), scripts 143/143 (the op-script guard covers the new script).

## Runbook
Revised by F-154 (2026-11-18) after the emulator rehearsal below. The op kind is `uc-season-rollover`, not `uc-script`: its dry run writes the backup path list to a file in the op's folder, the backup takes exactly that file, and the apply refuses any write the file does not cover.

**Before the day**
- Point-in-time recovery is ON for the production database (checked 2026-11-18: `POINT_IN_TIME_RECOVERY_ENABLED`, 7-day retention, location nam5). There are no scheduled backups and no managed backups. So the op's own backup plus PITR is the safety net, and PITR only reaches 7 days back: a mistake noticed later than that has only the op backup. No Auth export is needed, because the rollover writes no Auth data.
- F-150's own prerequisites are live (checked 2026-11-18): `onUserDeleted` is deployed (v1, `user.delete`), from OP-229's tree df0a5f0, which contains #278. The `seasons.memberUserIds` array-contains index is READY in both collection and collection-group scope.

**On the day** (after Abu Dhabi, 2026-12-06, is scored and 24 h have passed)
1. `aidlc op new uc-season-rollover -t "Season rollover 2026 → 2027"`, then `aidlc op dryrun`. This is the second dry run the rehearsal requires, on the real finished season: the rehearsal ran on doctored data. Read the preconditions, every price, every champion and the calendar. The script refuses while any 2026 race is open, any completed race has no `raceScores` rows, results are held, or a 2026 Moonshot call is not settled or cancelled.
2. `aidlc op apply` right after the dry run, with nothing merged in between. If a team or league was created after the dry run, the apply refuses ("not in the backup list") and writes nothing. Plan a new op and repeat.
3. Check: `config/app.currentSeason == '2027'`, a sample team reset with its `seasons/2026` archive, the 24 `*_2027` races, and the market showing the new prices once the market cache refreshes (30 min).

**Order of writes, and the point of no return**
The groups commit in this order: the season pin, all prices, one group per team, one group per league, the 2027 calendar, the season switch.
- Prices go first, so no reset team ever holds a fresh $1000 with 2026 prices on the market. A 2026 team that is not reset yet sees 2027 prices for a few seconds, but its reset follows.
- **Up to the season switch**, `aidlc op rollback` returns the database exactly to its state before the apply. The rehearsal proved this three times by comparing a digest of every document the rollover touches. A re-run instead finishes a stopped run. A failed group is reported and skipped, the season switch is held back, and committed groups are skipped on the re-run.
- **The point of no return is the first player action after the season switch.** From then on, a rollback would also wipe that player's new 2027 team (it overwrites teams with their 2026 state). The rollback stays mechanically possible until then. After it, **fix forward**: correct the specific documents with a reviewed uc-script op, never with a rollback. For example, re-price with a new op, or restore one team's archive into a new field. Players' 2027 picks stand.

**Rehearsal (F-154, 2026-11-18, Firestore emulator on the build box)**
- Copy: 915 documents read from production into a 0700 folder on the ops share (`/mnt/smb/f1-app/aidlc-ops/rehearsal-F-154/`). The copy covers teams, leagues and members, drivers, constructors, races, raceScores, pendingResults, moonshots, moonshotTokens, seasons and config/app. It leaves out `users` and notifications, which the rollover does not read. It was loaded into the emulator with `firestore-restore.js` (`FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST` set).
- Doctoring: `scripts/ops/rehearsal-doctor.js`, which refuses unless `FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST` is loopback. It completed Las Vegas, Qatar and Abu Dhabi with race times 48 h back, and gave each a copy of Brazil's 33 raceScores rows.
- Switch: `seasonRollover.js --emulator=127.0.0.1:PORT` never loads the service-account key. A production run is refused while `FIRESTORE_EMULATOR_HOST` is set. Both are tested in `functions/src/season/rolloverConnection.ts`.
- **First apply: every one of the 98 team groups failed**, with "Can't merge current value into a nested object". `update()` cannot write a top-level field named `constructor`: the SDK finds `Object.prototype.constructor` while it expands field paths, the same trap F-141 hit in auto-fill. In production this would have committed prices, leagues and the calendar, reset no team, and held the season switch. **Fixed:** the team reset is now written with `set(…, { mergeFields })`, beside an `update()` of `updatedAt` that fails the group if the team was deleted mid-run. A test checks that no plan `update()` carries `constructor`.
- After the fix:
  - **Full run:** 129 groups. 98 teams archived and reset, 27 leagues archived and reset with 39 members zeroed, 34 prices, 24 races of 2027, season switched. A second run refuses ("already rolled over").
  - **Run stopped dead after 60 groups** (`--fail-after=60`, rehearsal only): prices all in, 58 teams reset, no league or 2027 race, still 2026. The re-run reset the 40 teams left, and its end state matched the full run's digest.
  - **Rollback** with the op's restore and the same path file: back to the pre-apply digest each time (three times).
- The copy stays on the share for F-155/F-157 and is deleted when they are done.

**What the rollover does to Track Limits** (read from /data/tracklimits at c41b084, 2026-11-18)
- Prices: Track Limits buys drivers and constructors with garage cash at Undercut's `price` and `tier` (`functions/src/economy/garageCallables.ts`). The rollover lowers prices about 46% (active drivers average $296 today → $160; constructors $409 → $163 on the rehearsal copy) and recomputes tiers. Everything in a Track Limits garage gets cheaper overnight. That is that app's owner's call, so tell them before the day.
- `fantasyPoints` goes to 0, and Track Limits shows it on its driver and constructor cards. `seasonPoints` holds the 2026 total.
- Races: Track Limits picks its next race by date across all seasons (`src/services/data.service.ts`), so after the rollover the 2027 opener is next. That is correct, because the rollover runs after the 2026 finale. Its missing-picks push only looks 24 h ahead. Its season history queries take a season id.
- `tlSeedRaces` writes a 2026 calendar from a JSON file. It must not be run for 2027; the rollover creates 2027.

## Data
User data: team names, league names, member user ids and display names are copied into the archive
documents under the same team and league. The new `seasons` subcollections match no rule, so no
client can read or write them until a later feature opens them (it must leave out departed members).
Account deletion removes a user's team archives and anonymises their rows in league archives. Nothing
leaves Firestore; the op's log and backup land on `/mnt/smb/f1-app/aidlc-ops`, as for every op. The
2027 calendar is OpenF1 data, used under the same owner decision as live timing (ADR-001 note).

## Performance budget
One-off op: about 97 teams, 27 leagues, 23 drivers, 11 constructors, 24 races — a few hundred writes in batches of 400.

## Out of scope
- The price level: decided 2026-11-15 by the owner — $6 per point for drivers, $3 for constructors, so 2027 opens at 2026's level (set in F-151).
- Showing past seasons in the app (the archives are written now; reading them is a later feature).
- `seasonId` on teams and leagues stays client-writable and means nothing to the server; nothing should trust it. New teams after the rollover carry none.
- A league its owner deletes keeps its `seasons` archive (anonymised when a member deletes their account); removing archives with their league is a later change.
- A stand-in's results count toward the seat holder's price only if they were scored under the holder's id (Hirakawa's and Câmara's were their own).
- The 2027 driver lineup and car numbers; the app and portal changes listed above.
