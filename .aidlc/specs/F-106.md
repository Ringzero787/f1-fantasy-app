# F-106 — UC: Moonshot config, pricing and calls

Created: 2026-10-06 · Status: planned · Design source: `docs/design/moonshot/SPEC.md` §1–§15, §23–§27, §31, §33

## Goal
Undercut (root app). Moonshot is the second-half comeback mechanic: a manager stakes Championship Points or Cash on a prediction about one driver's race result and is paid a multiplier if it hits. This feature is the server side: the configuration that governs it, the probability snapshot a call is priced from, and the callables that quote, confirm, change and cancel a call before lock. Everything the client shows comes from the server; the client is trusted for nothing but the choice.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `config/app.moonshot` block, read by functions and clients, fail-closed (absent or `enabled: false` means no Moonshot anywhere): `enabled`, `unlockRound`, `tokensPerTeam` (3), `maxPerRace` (1), `predictionTypesEnabled` (V1: `TOP_5`, `PODIUM`, `WIN`; `EXACT_FINISH` present but off until the F-070 calibration gate has passed), `pointsStakeLevels` [50,100,200], `cashStakeLevels`, `pricing` (`mode: banded | continuous`, `bands[]` as in SPEC §7, `vig`, `rounding`, `maxMultiplier` 8), `maxSingleRewardPoints` 1000, `maxSeasonMoonshotPointGain` 1500, `dnfRule` LOSS, `dnsRule` VOID, `dsqRule` LOSS, `cancelledRule` VOID, `hideBeforeLock` true, `tutorialEnabled`, `copy` (the strings in SPEC §2, §8, §13, §20). Changed through `uc-script` ops only.
- [ ] **Probability snapshot.** The F-070 worker publishes, per driver per round and per refresh, the full finishing-position distribution (`positions: number[20]`, summing to 1) alongside today's `win/pod/t10`, as `pw_moonshot_odds/{season}_{round}_{sessionKey}` with a `modelVersion`. A quote is priced from the latest snapshot at quote time and the Moonshot stores `probabilitySnapshotId`, `modelVersion` and `modelProbability`; the multiplier never changes afterwards (SPEC §14).
- [ ] **Pricing.** `banded`: the probability falls into a configured band and takes its label and multiplier. `continuous`: `multiplier = round((1 − p) / p × (1 − vig), rounding)`, floored at 0.25, capped at `maxMultiplier`, labelled by the same bands. Pure function with tests at every band edge; the spec records the expected value per stake at each band edge under `banded` (SAFE 85%: +0.35; BOLD 65%: +0.46; LONGSHOT 40%: +0.40; MOONSHOT 20%: +0.20; EXTREME 8%: −0.28) so the launch simulation (SPEC §33) can choose.
- [ ] **Callables** (`functions/src/moonshot/`): `moonshotQuote({ raceId, driverId, predictionType, predictionTarget?, stakeCurrency, stakeAmount })` → `{ quoteId, modelProbability, rewardBand, multiplier, stakeAmount, potentialReward, expiresAt }` (quote valid 10 minutes); `moonshotConfirm({ quoteId })` creates `moonshots/{id}` in `CONFIRMED` with every field of SPEC §23; `moonshotCancel({ moonshotId })` before lock only, returning the token. Each verifies: feature enabled and round ≥ unlock; caller owns the team; tokens remaining; no other Moonshot on this race for the team; driver on the entry list; stake in the configured levels and within balance (points: season total; cash: bank); reward ≤ `maxSingleRewardPoints`; the race's lock time not passed (same lock as lineups, or `lockOverride`). Lock is enforced server-side: a confirmed Moonshot flips to `LOCKED` by the existing lock sweep and rejects changes after.
- [ ] **Double Down** is allowed (driver may be on the roster; the quote marks `ownsDriver: true` for the client's framing). A driver with a grid penalty published after the snapshot is not quotable until the next snapshot.
- [ ] Rules: `moonshots` is readable by the owner and, once `LOCKED` or later, by members of the team's league (`hideBeforeLock`); never client-writable. `pw_moonshot_odds` is readable by any signed-in user (pricing must be visible to everyone — it is not a pass feature).
- [ ] Unit tests: pricing (both modes, every band edge, cap and floor), eligibility decisions as a pure function (enabled/round/tokens/per-race/stake/balance/lock), quote expiry, idempotent confirm (a quoteId confirms once). Deploys through `aidlc op functions-deploy` and `rules-deploy`.

## Targets
- firebase (functions, rules)

## Data
New `moonshots` collection: the team's calls with stake, prediction, snapshot and (later) result; readable by league members after lock. Analytics events of SPEC §32 for the server-side steps (`moonshot_quote_viewed` is client-side, `moonshot_confirmed/cancelled/locked` here).

## Performance budget
Quote: two reads (snapshot, team) and no writes. Confirm: one transaction. Snapshot publish adds one document per refresh.

## Out of scope
- Settlement, standings and feed (F-107); app and portal surfaces (F-108, F-109).
- Head-to-head, multi-driver, qualifying or fastest-lap predictions; Final Moonshot (SPEC §31 keys reserved, not built).
