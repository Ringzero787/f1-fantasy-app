# F-166 — UC: one-blip silence for Pit Wall worker emails

Created: 2026-11-20 · Status: implemented

## Goal
At 21:39 UTC on 2026-11-20 one Firestore call returned `14 UNAVAILABLE` and the next tick, at 21:45, was fine. The owner still got two emails: "could not reach the database", then "is back (was away 14 minutes)". Neither asked them to do anything. A single network failure that the next tick recovers from should be recorded and not mailed.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A failed tick whose error is a network kind (F-153 `classifyTickError`) is mailed only from the second failure in a row (`emailTickFailure`, `onTickFailure`, alertText.test.js). A `--once` run, which has no next tick, always mails.
- [ ] A deadline failure (a tick hung for 25 minutes) and an error of an unknown kind are still mailed on the first failure.
- [ ] A failure that is not mailed is still written to `pw_alerts`, so the ops check lists it, and to the journal.
- [ ] A failure that is not mailed does not arm the "is back" email (`onTickFailure`, tested). The heartbeat-gap all-clear (more than 30 minutes without a heartbeat) is unchanged.
- [ ] The restart after three failures in a row is unchanged.

## Targets
- The Pit Wall worker on forge (`workers/pitwall`). It goes live with a `pitwall-worker-deploy` op, which the owner applies after the Las Vegas race weekend. No change to the app, functions or rules.

## Data
None. The emails carry the same fields as before.

n/a.

## Out of scope
- Rate-limiting the job-failed and stale-data emails. Each of those already means something needs a look.
