# F-119 — UC: Pit Wall says who to ace in the app, and the driver sheet scrolls

Created: 2026-10-09 · Status: shipped in 2.5.2 · Reported from the field by the owner, 2026-10-09, with a
screenshot: "the data rolls off the screen and it's not scrollable. We also don't have any advice
if I should or should not ace this driver. Looking at the high level, ace is clear it wasn't set
but didn't recommend with Pit Wall (which the website does a great job of)."

## Goal
Undercut (app only; no server, rules or portal change). Two things a pass holder met on the driver
sheet. First, on iOS the sheet did not scroll at all, and since F-088 put the Pit Wall block in it
the sheet is taller than the screen — so the stats, the contract and the Ace and Remove buttons
were out of reach. Second, the app showed each driver's projection but never said which of them
should carry the ace; the portal's Briefing has said that since F-097. The Team screen said only
"no ace set". Both are fixed where the player already is: the sheet scrolls, the sheet says whether
this driver is the one to ace, and the Team screen names the driver Pit Wall would ace.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **The driver sheet scrolls on iOS.** Reproduced on the iOS simulator on the 2.5.1 code: a
      swipe up inside the open sheet left the screen pixel-identical
      (`docs/design/grid/verify/F-119-ios-before-swipe-does-nothing.png`). With the change the same
      swipe reaches the Ace and Remove buttons (`F-119-ios-sheet-scrolled.png`). Android scrolled
      before and was not the bug.
- [x] **The cause is the wrapper, and the other sheets built the same way get the same change.**
      The scrim was a `Pressable` around a second `Pressable` around the `ScrollView`. The scrim is
      now a sibling behind the sheet (`GridTileSheet`, `GridMoonshotSheet`, `GridRaceSelectSheet`).
      Tapping the scrim still closes the sheet; tapping the sheet does not (verified on the
      simulator).
- [x] **One rule for who carries the ace, the portal's.** `aceAdvice` in
      `src/pitwall/aceAdvice.ts`: the highest projection among the team's drivers that may carry
      the ace, as `briefRecs` in `web/pitwall/src/data/logic.ts`. Eligibility is the tile's own
      answer on the ace cap, so it never names a driver the save would refuse (tests).
      **Where it differs from the portal, on purpose:** with no ace set the portal says nothing and
      the app names one (the owner's report was exactly that silence); level projections are a keep
      here where the portal can say "move, +0"; and a constructor ace that out-projects every
      eligible driver gets silence here where the portal says move at a loss.
- [x] **An ace that has risen over the cap is told to move**, whatever it projects: scoring strips
      the multiplier above the cap, so it is doubling nothing. The first cut of this stayed silent
      there (found by the local quality review); the holder's own eligibility is now an input, for
      a driver or a constructor (tests).
- [x] **Compared in the whole points the screen shows.** Projections are rounded once on the way
      in, so a fraction ahead is level and no line can read "+0" or "0 clear" (tests).
- [x] **Never from last round's projections.** After a race is scored the ace reopens before the
      worker publishes the next round; `projectionsAreForRound` holds the advice back until the
      set's round is the round about to be played. Unknown on either side is not a mismatch, and
      the showcase's fixed round is exempt (tests for the pure rule).
- [x] **Three answers: set, move, keep.** No ace → name the driver and the projection, doubled.
      Ace on a lower projection → move it, with the gain before doubling. Ace already on the best →
      keep it, with the lead over the next best. Level projections are a keep, not a move (tests).
- [x] **Silent when there is nothing to reason from**: no pass (no projections exist in the store),
      no eligible driver with a projection, a constructor ace that out-projects every eligible
      driver, or the ace is locked for the round — advice the player cannot act on is noise (tests
      for the pure cases).
- [x] **The Team screen says it under the ace status** (`F-119-ios-team-ace-line.png`), and **the
      sheet says it first in the Pit Wall block** as ACE CALL: this driver, or not this one and who
      instead, or over the cap (`F-119-ios-sheet-ace-call.png`). A member viewing someone else's
      team sees neither.
- [x] **The restructured sheets keep their accessibility shape**: the scrim is a button labelled
      Close, and the race-select sheet's container stays out of the focus order as before. Not
      checked with VoiceOver or TalkBack.

## Targets
- android
- ios

## Data
No user data. The advice is computed on the device from the projections a pass holder already
downloads and the team already on screen; nothing new is read, stored or sent.

## Performance budget
One sort of at most five drivers per render of the Team screen, memoised. Bundle +3 KB source.

## Out of scope
- Recommending the constructor as ace. The portal does not, and two surfaces giving different
  advice is worse than either rule.
- The legacy `(tabs)` UI.
- Component tests for the wiring in `GridTeamPanel` (ace locked, member view, constructor sheet):
  the repo has no React Native render harness; these were checked by reading and on the simulator.
- The Moonshot and race-select sheets were restructured the same way and only typechecked and
  linted in the PR. Both were driven on the iOS simulator on the merged commit before the 2.5.2
  build (`release-notes-2.5.2.md`); neither was driven on Android.
- A one-tap "apply" on the Team screen line; the tile's ACE chip is one tap away.
- Shipping: this needs an app build (2.5.2) on all three stores.
