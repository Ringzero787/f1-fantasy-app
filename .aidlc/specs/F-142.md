# F-142 — UC: Owner playtest fixes (market buy and details, team-first names, Moonshot feed, suffix surnames, car numbers)

Created: 2026-11-13 · Status: building · App: Undercut (app only, next store build)

## Goal

The owner's 2026-11-13 playtest list. Every item ships in the next build; there is no server or rules change.

1. **A $5 constructor could not be bought with $18 in the bank.** The picker blocked any row that left less than the cheapest finish of the lineup (the reserve from #238). With open driver seats, Cadillac at $5 plus the two cheapest drivers came to more than $18, so the row was dimmed and untappable. But partial lineups save and fill at lock (F-044), so the reserve should be advice, not a wall.
2. **Market rows could not be opened outside edit mode.** While the lineup is locked, and on any blocked row, the row was `disabled`. The picker had no way to see a driver's details.
3. **The league Moonshot feed named the player and ran long** (stake, at risk, model chance, reward, gain). The owner wants "Moonshot +X points · team name".
4. **Team name and player name were the wrong way round** in the league table and the round report: the player's name was the headline and the team the small line. The owner wants the team large and the real name small, everywhere.
5. **Sainz showed as "JR."** The surname helper took the last word of "Carlos Sainz Jr.".
6. **Car numbers were small and grey** on the team tile and the member (league) tile. The owner wants them a little larger and in the team colour.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Picker blocking:**
  - A row is blocked only by the bank (price over what is left), a full driver line-up, or a sell lock-out.
  - A row that fits the bank but leaves less than the cheapest finish is selectable. It is marked `TIGHT · LEAVES TOO LITTLE FOR THE REST`, and Pit Wall never marks it as a pick, a best value or a pairing hint.
  - The save summary's existing open-seats warning still shows.
- [ ] **Market details:**
  - Every market row opens a read-only details sheet when the lineup is locked or the row is blocked.
  - Any row opens the sheet on a long press.
  - The sheet shows season points, last race, market price and form, with no contract, purchase price or sell block.
  - A blocked row's sheet says why (over the bank by $N, line-up full, or locked out).
- [ ] **League Moonshot feed:** one line per call: `🚀 MOONSHOT +1,000 POINTS · TEAM` for a called shot (what it pays), and `🔥/💥 MOONSHOT ±N POINTS · TEAM` or `↩ MOONSHOT VOID · TEAM` for a settled one. Tested: `__tests__/simple/f142Names.test.ts`.
- [ ] **Team first, player second:**
  - League table and round report (podium, callouts, Moonshots, share card): the headline is the team name and the player's name is the small line. A member with no team name falls back to the player's name with no second line.
  - Race wins stay on the small line.
  - The shared standings text is unchanged.
  - The race-day Moonshot list names teams.
  - Tested: `f142Names.test.ts`.
- [ ] **Suffix surnames:** `surnameOf` skips Jr./Sr./II/III/IV ("Carlos Sainz Jr." → "Sainz"), and the weekend recap uses it. Tested.
- [ ] **Car numbers:** the car number on the team and member tiles is 13 px, extra-bold and in the team's accent colour. So is the number on picker rows.
- [ ] Typecheck and the full jest suite pass.
