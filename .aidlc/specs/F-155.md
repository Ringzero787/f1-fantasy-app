# F-155 — UC: off-season state for the app, the market and the portal after the rollover

Created: 2026-11-17 · Status: building

## Goal

After the rollover, every team is empty, there is no race for about 14 weeks, and the driver list is still 2026's. Nothing says what a player sees or may do in that time. The market stays open, so teams can be rebuilt on a grid that will change; `setDriverSeat.js` refuses to retire a driver a team holds; the newcomer price rule in the rollover prices a mid-season stand-in as a newcomer; and the portal (`web/pitwall/src/lib/env.ts`, season pinned) would go on showing the last 2026 round's projections against reset teams.

Source: the 2026-11-17 EGM review of 2027 readiness (finding R-06, R-22); the evidence lines were re-checked against `origin/master` on that date. Dates are proposals worked back from the 2027-03-14 opener, not owner decisions, unless marked.

## Newcomer price
- Before: fewer than 5 scored races → $5. On 2026's data that priced Câmara (2 races, 12 points; Ocon's Haas seat from São Paulo) at $5.
- Now: fewer than 5 scored races → the median opening price of the active drivers priced from their own results (inactive drivers are re-priced but left out of the median). That covers a driver with no 2026 points at all and a stand-in with a race or two. Hirakawa (car 50, Mexico City, 1 race) has raceScores under his own id but no `drivers` document, so the rollover does not price him; if F-156 adds him he is new in 2027 and opens at `seasons/2027.newcomerPrice`, which the season switch now writes for F-156 to read. Constructors get the median constructor the same way (none qualifies on 2026's data).
- **Which median: the 2027 opening prices (owner, 2026-11-24).** On 2026-11-24's data that is **$150** (Lindblad, tier B). The median of today's prices of the same 21 drivers would be $244 (tier A): today's prices ran up through the season while the rollover opens 2027 at 2026's opening level, so $244 would open a driver with no results as the ninth dearest. The dry run prints both figures.
- Tests: `functions/test/seasonRollover.test.js` (median, newcomer on 2026-11-24's 21 drivers, re-run stability, fallback).

## Retire with refund
- `setDriverSeat.js` was extended rather than a new script added. Retiring a held driver and refunding his holders are one change: split across two ops, teams could hold an inactive driver the scorer still scores, or be refunded for a driver the market still sells. One op gives one backup and one rollback. `add: null` now retires with no replacement; a new driver given no price opens at `seasons/<current season>.newcomerPrice` (refused if absent).
- **Backup:** the dry run prints the op's `backup=` line: `drivers/<retire>`, `drivers/<add>` and the holding teams' documents only. Never the whole `fantasyTeams` collection: the market is open, and restoring it would undo every other player's changes. A team that buys him after the dry run is still refunded, and the apply names it as outside the backup.
- `refundHolders: true` in `DESIRED`, committed before the op (the uc-script op passes only `--apply`). The apply retires him first (`isActive: false`, so nobody can buy him again; a re-run keeps the first `retiredAt`), **then reads the holders again** (a team that bought him between the first read and the retirement is refunded too), then writes one transaction per holding team. Inside it the live weekend marker and the team's `isLocked` are read again, as `assertOwnedUnlockedTeam` does: a locked team is skipped and reported, and a weekend gone live stops the run. Each write takes him off the roster, his current market price credited whole (no early-termination fee; the player did not break the contract), totalSpent less his purchase price, his points banked from totalPoints to lockedPoints as in a sale (totals and league tables unchanged), ace, lockout and streak entries cleared. Written with `set` + `mergeFields` over literal `FieldPath`s, never `update()` (#305). A deleted team is not recreated. A failed team is reported, and a re-run touches only teams still holding him.
- `racesSinceTransfer` is left alone: it is the stale-roster counter, and a sale the server forces says nothing about whether the player is active. Resetting it would hand an abandoned team a reprieve. The player's own purchase into the empty seat resets it, as does auto-fill at the lock (F-170), and in the off-season it is 0 on every team anyway.
- The app keeps no sale history, so there is none to add to.
- Refuses to start while a weekend is live (`config/lockState`) or he has an open Moonshot. A run with a skipped or failed team exits non-zero; a re-run touches only teams still holding him.
- Tests: `functions/test/retireDriver.test.js` (9), `functions/test/emulator/retireDriver.emu.test.js` (10, real emulator, `demo-f155-retire`): a team carrying a `constructor` object, `constructor: null`, lockout-only, an entry matched on `id`, a non-holder, a locked team skipped while the others are written, a weekend going live mid-run, a buyer between read and retirement, the newcomer price for an added driver, `retiredAt` kept on a re-run, `add: null`.

## The reset notice
Channels: 66 real players (125 users, 59 test accounts). Only 10 hold a push token, all from the old tabs UI, because the Grid UI registers none. The in-app inbox is shown only in that old UI. Email reaches every player, but Undercut sends it only from Cloud Functions (the Gmail secret is not on the build box). So:
1. `sendSeasonNotice.js` (uc-script op): inbox + push, test accounts excluded, once per user. Each batch's pushes go out right after it commits; failures are counted, and `seasons/2026.resetNotice` is always stamped with the real counts (`sentAt` once anything went out). Set `RESET_DAY` first; the apply refuses the placeholder.
2. The same title and body by email from the admin page's broadcast ("send email" ticked). After the functions deploy below, it skips test accounts. Then the owner stamps it: `stampSeasonNoticeEmail.js` (uc-script op) writes only `resetNotice.emailAt`, and refuses before step 1. It is a script of its own because the uc-script op passes no flag but `--apply`.
3. For 2.8.0 players, once 2.9.0 is live on all three stores: `latestVersion` 2.9.0 with an `updateMessage` naming the reset is an honest nudge on the next cold start. This is optional, and it is not used for 2.9.0 players, because it would show a false update.

**Title:** Undercut 2027: every team starts fresh

**Body:** The 2026 season is over. On <RESET DAY> every Undercut team starts again for 2027: an empty lineup, a $1,000 budget and zero points. Your team keeps its name and stays in its leagues. Your 2026 points and every league's final table and champion are saved. Driver prices are reset from their 2026 results, and the market stays open all winter, so you can build your 2027 lineup straight away. If a driver you pick has no seat when the 2027 grid is confirmed, we take him off your team and give you back his price. Round 1 is Australia on 14 March 2027. Your lineup locks at qualifying the day before.

## Ops, in order
1. `functions-deploy` with `only=functions:sendGlobalBroadcast` (the test-account filter). This is needed only for the email.
2. `pitwall-pages-deploy` from a master that carries this feature. The off-season state then switches on by itself at the rollover.
3. Commit `RESET_DAY` in `sendSeasonNotice.js`, then a `uc-script` op: `script=sendSeasonNotice.js`, `backup=seasons/2026`. Dry run, then apply, at least 24 h before the rollover.
4. Owner: the admin page broadcast with email, same text; then a `uc-script` op `script=stampSeasonNoticeEmail.js backup=seasons/2026`.
5. Optional, once 2.9.0 is live everywhere: `latestVersion` + `updateMessage` (`setAppVersionGate.js` op).
6. `uc-season-rollover` (F-150 runbook). The dry run now prints the newcomer price, and the apply refuses until step 3 is 24 h old and step 4's stamp exists.
7. Straight after: refresh the market cache (`forceRefreshMarketCache`, or wait 30 min) before telling anyone.
8. January (F-156): one `uc-script` op per seat change, `setDriverSeat.js` with `refundHolders: true`. Dry-run it first on its own (`node scripts/ops/run-script.js undercut setDriverSeat.js`) to get the backup line, then `backup=` that line: `drivers/<retire>,drivers/<add>,fantasyTeams/<each holder>`.
