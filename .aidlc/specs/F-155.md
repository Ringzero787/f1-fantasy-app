# F-155 — UC: off-season state for the app, the market and the portal after the rollover

Created: 2026-11-17 · Status: planned

## Goal

After the rollover, every team is empty, there is no race for about 14 weeks, and the driver list is still 2026's. Nothing says what a player sees or may do in that time. The market stays open, so teams can be rebuilt on a grid that will change; `setDriverSeat.js` refuses to retire a driver a team holds; the newcomer price rule in the rollover prices a mid-season stand-in as a newcomer; and the portal (`web/pitwall/src/lib/env.ts`, season pinned) would go on showing the last 2026 round's projections against reset teams.

Source: the 2026-11-17 EGM review of 2027 readiness (finding R-06, R-22); the evidence lines were re-checked against `origin/master` on that date. Dates are proposals worked back from the 2027-03-14 opener, not owner decisions, unless marked.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Owner decision recorded:** the market is open or closed between the rollover and the 2027 grid seed (F-156).
- [ ] **Owner decision recorded:** the newcomer price rule, and which drivers it applies to.
- [ ] If closed: roster and market writes are refused server-side with a message, and the app says why; covered by a rules or callable test.
- [ ] The Team screen for a reset team with no upcoming race says the season is over and when the next one starts, instead of an empty lineup with a countdown to nothing.
- [ ] The portal shows an explicit off-season state from the day of the rollover.
- [ ] Players are told about the reset before it happens (push or in-app notice; the wording is in this spec once written).
- [ ] **The rollover (F-150) does not run until the server-side parts of this feature are live** — the market decision enforced, the portal's off-season state, the notice sent. If they cannot be ready by the first possible rollover day, the rollover waits: nothing forces it to run on 2026-12-07.
