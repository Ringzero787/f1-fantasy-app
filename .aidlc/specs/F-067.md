# F-067 — UC: U-mark app icon and launch reveal

Created: 2026-09-19 · Status: building

## Goal

The Grid redesign shipped with the old white-and-teal car icon. The owner chose a new mark: a white U in the app's heading face with a red diagonal cut across its lower right, on the app's near-black ground. Because a lone U does not say "Undercut" on its own, a cold start shows that U and unrolls the rest of the UNDERCUT wordmark out of it before the app appears.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `assets/icon.png`, `assets/adaptive-icon.png` (mark inside the 66% safe zone, transparent), `assets/favicon.png` and `assets/splash.png` are the U mark; splash and adaptive-icon background are `#0E0E0E` in `app.config.js`. All generated by `scripts/design/make-u-mark.py` from Unbounded Black (OFL), which also writes the 1024/512/114 store icons to a directory given on the command line.
- [ ] `LaunchReveal` (mounted once in `app/_layout.tsx`, above everything) plays on a cold start: the U at splash size, then the letters unroll to the right while the row shrinks, the full wordmark holds, and the overlay fades into the app. Total under two seconds. The two wordmark halves are pre-rendered PNGs, so the reveal does not wait for fonts.
- [ ] The unrolling wordmark never passes 90% of the screen width and lands on scale 1; the wordmark is 78% of the screen width, capped at 520 on tablets; the U starts as wide as it is on the native splash (30% of the short side).
- [ ] Reduced motion (OS setting): no movement, the full wordmark holds briefly and fades.
- [ ] A failsafe removes the overlay if an animation callback is dropped; the overlay blocks touches only while visible.
- [ ] Sizing and timing are pure functions in `src/simple/grid/launchReveal.ts` with jest coverage. `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx jest --ci` pass. Frames in `docs/design/grid/verify/F-067-*`.

## Targets
- android
- ios

## Data
None.

## Performance budget
Two small PNGs (under 40 KB together) and one JS-driven animation of about 650 ms on cold start only. The app mounts and loads underneath the overlay, so time to an interactive screen is unchanged apart from the overlay's own duration.

## Out of scope
- A native animated splash (Android 12 AnimatedVectorDrawable, iOS storyboard animation).
- Replaying the reveal on warm resumes.
