# F-170 — UC: auto-fill resets the stale-roster counter like a transfer

Created: 2026-11-24 · Status: shipped

## Goal
A team that makes no transfer for more than five races takes a stale-roster penalty of (racesSinceTransfer − 5) × 5 points a race (`functions/src/scoring/calculatePoints.ts`). A lock-time auto-fill (F-044, F-141) buys a driver or a constructor into an empty seat, but it did not reset `racesSinceTransfer`. So an abandoned team that auto-fill kept full was penalised more every race. At Las Vegas 2026 that was 58 teams, every one of them auto-filled, for −2675 points (worst −75).

Owner decision, 2026-11-24: auto-fill counts as a transfer.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A fill writes `racesSinceTransfer: 0` in the same `set` + `mergeFields` write as the roster (`autoFillWrite`, `functions/src/teams/autoFill.ts`). Test: `functions/test/autoFillWrite.test.js`, which fails on the old code.
- [x] The order holds. Auto-fill runs in the lock sweep, before the race is scored. Scoring reads the counter, takes the penalty from it, then increments it. A team filled at the lock therefore takes no penalty that race and counts again from 1, as after a player's own purchase.
- [x] A team that auto-fill does not touch (no empty seat, never fielded, or locked) keeps its counter: no write at all. The lock sweep is the only caller of a fill, which is the ordering the criterion above rests on. Both are tested in `functions/test/autoFillWrite.test.js`.
- [x] Deployed: `functions-deploy only=functions:autoLockTeams`, before the Qatar lock (R24, race 2026-11-29). OP-244, applied 2026-11-24.
- [x] Penalties already taken by Vegas-filled teams are refunded by `refundStalePenalties.js` (owner decision 2026-11-24), applied as a uc-script op before the Qatar lock. A team is in scope when its server-written `las_vegas_2026` snapshot holds a pick the lock sweep added (`isReservePick: true`, `racesHeld: 0`), or when `lastAutoFill` names Las Vegas; a `lastAutoFill` that the snapshot does not confirm is INELIGIBLE. For each eligible team, the script refunds: **Done:** OP-247, applied 2026-11-24: +17,115 points to 60 teams (13,700 recorded, 3,415 rebuilt), counters reset on 91, 16 leagues re-synced, 5 Azerbaijan entries left for review; a read-only rerun found nothing left to refund.
  - every 2026 race-phase snapshot `adjustment` shaped like a stale penalty (negative, a multiple of 5, equal to points − Σentities, race points not 0, no driver listed twice, consistent with the neighbouring snapshots);
  - plus, before the first snapshot, the penalties reconstructed from the counter that snapshot implies, walking back through the team's scored 2026 races, for races run on or after 2026-06-13 only. Before 2df4a16 the counter and `scoredRaces` were client-writable, and the repair paths added race ids to `scoredRaces` without moving the counter. This also covers the 19 teams of the 2026-05-30 de-doubling repair. The reconstruction runs only when today's `racesSinceTransfer` equals the counter at the latest penalised snapshot plus the scored races from it on (the near-end cross-check), every race in the walk is a completed race doc, and no 2026 id in `scoredRaces` lacks a race doc.
  - Every eligible team's `racesSinceTransfer` becomes min(current, 1), the value the F-170 rule gives, whatever the refund.
  - GAP (scored races neither snapshotted nor reconstructed), UNEXPLAINED (adjustments or counters that do not fit) and INELIGIBLE entries are listed in the dry run for the owner and are not refunded.
  - `--apply` is refused once a race after Las Vegas has completed.
- [x] `lastAutoFill` and `staleRefund` are server-only on `fantasyTeams` (firestore.rules, create and update), deployed as a rules op BEFORE the refund is applied. Test: `rules-tests/firestore.rules.test.js`. **Done:** OP-246, applied 2026-11-24 before OP-247.

## Targets
- Undercut functions: `autoLockTeams`.
- Firestore rules: `lastAutoFill` and `staleRefund` join the `fantasyTeams` server-owned fields (the refund trusts the first and is keyed on the second).
- Repair: `functions/scripts/refundStalePenalties.js` (uc-script op).
- No app or copy change: no player-facing text describes the penalty.

## Data
`fantasyTeams.racesSinceTransfer` is written by the lock sweep on teams it fills. No personal data.

The refund (`refundStalePenalties.js --apply`) writes, per refunded team, in one transaction:
- `fantasyTeams/{id}.totalPoints`: plus the refund.
- `fantasyTeams/{id}.racesSinceTransfer`: min(current, 1), for every eligible team, even one with nothing to refund.
- `fantasyTeams/{id}.staleRefund`: `{ at, races: { raceId: points }, total, counterBefore }`: the penalties reconstructed from the counter for races before the first snapshot (merged with an earlier run's), and the counter before this repair first reset it (the near-end cross-check reads it on a rerun). Written with any of the team writes above.
- `fantasyTeams/{id}/raceSnapshots/{raceId}.staleRefund`: `{ at, points }` on each snapshot whose penalty was refunded.

Then, through `syncLeaguesForRace` for the latest completed race, for every league of a refunded team: `leagues/{id}/members/*` (`totalPoints`, `lastRacePoints`, `lastRaceId`, `moonshotPoints`, `rank`, `raceWins`), `leagues/{id}/raceResults/{raceId}` (rewritten with the same numbers) and `leagues/{id}.raceResultIds`. Snapshot phases, `bestRacePoints` and earlier weekend leaderboards are not changed. Nothing of Track Limits.

## Performance budget
The forward fix adds one field to a write that already happens. The refund is a one-off repair op over about 100 teams: one `getAll` of their Las Vegas snapshots, then one transaction per in-scope team, then one league sync.

## Out of scope
- Changing the penalty's size or threshold.
