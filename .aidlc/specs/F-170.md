# F-170 — UC: auto-fill resets the stale-roster counter like a transfer

Created: 2026-11-24 · Status: shipped

## Goal
A team that makes no transfer for more than five races takes a stale-roster penalty of (racesSinceTransfer − 5) × 5 points a race (`functions/src/scoring/calculatePoints.ts`). A lock-time auto-fill (F-044, F-141) buys a driver or a constructor into an empty seat, but it did not reset `racesSinceTransfer`. So an abandoned team that auto-fill kept full was penalised more every race. At Las Vegas 2026 that was 58 teams, every one of them auto-filled, for −2675 points (worst −75).

Owner decision, 2026-11-24: auto-fill counts as a transfer.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] A fill writes `racesSinceTransfer: 0` in the same `set` + `mergeFields` write as the roster (`autoFillWrite`, `functions/src/teams/autoFill.ts`). Test: `functions/test/autoFillWrite.test.js`, which fails on the old code.
- [x] The order holds. Auto-fill runs in the lock sweep, before the race is scored. Scoring reads the counter, takes the penalty from it, then increments it. A team filled at the lock therefore takes no penalty that race and counts again from 1, as after a player's own purchase.
- [x] A team that auto-fill does not touch (no empty seat, never fielded, or locked) keeps its counter: no write at all. The lock sweep is the only caller of a fill, which is the ordering the criterion above rests on. Both are tested in `functions/test/autoFillWrite.test.js`.
- [x] Deployed: `functions-deploy only=functions:autoLockTeams`, before the Qatar lock (R24, race 2026-11-29). OP-244, applied 2026-11-24.
- [ ] Penalties already taken by Vegas-filled teams are refunded by `refundStalePenalties.js` (owner decision 2026-11-24), applied as a uc-script op before the Qatar lock.
- [ ] `lastAutoFill` and `staleRefund` are server-only on `fantasyTeams` (firestore.rules, create and update), deployed as a rules op BEFORE the refund is applied. Test: `rules-tests/firestore.rules.test.js`.

## Targets
- Undercut functions: `autoLockTeams`.
- Firestore rules: `lastAutoFill` and `staleRefund` join the `fantasyTeams` server-owned fields (the refund trusts the first and is keyed on the second).
- Repair: `functions/scripts/refundStalePenalties.js` (uc-script op).
- No app or copy change: no player-facing text describes the penalty.

## Data
`fantasyTeams.racesSinceTransfer` is written by the lock sweep on teams it fills. No personal data.

The refund (`refundStalePenalties.js --apply`) writes, per refunded team, in one transaction:
- `fantasyTeams/{id}.totalPoints`: plus the refund.
- `fantasyTeams/{id}.staleRefund`: `{ at, races: { raceId: points }, total }`, the penalties reconstructed from the counter for races before the first snapshot. Only when there are some; merged with an earlier run's.
- `fantasyTeams/{id}/raceSnapshots/{raceId}.staleRefund`: `{ at, points }` on each snapshot whose penalty was refunded.

Then, through `syncLeaguesForRace` for the latest completed race, for every league of a refunded team: `leagues/{id}/members/*` (`totalPoints`, `lastRacePoints`, `lastRaceId`, `moonshotPoints`, `rank`, `raceWins`), `leagues/{id}/raceResults/{raceId}` (rewritten with the same numbers) and `leagues/{id}.raceResultIds`. Snapshot phases, `bestRacePoints` and earlier weekend leaderboards are not changed. Nothing of Track Limits.

## Performance budget
None: one more field in a write that already happens.

## Out of scope
- Changing the penalty's size or threshold.
