# F-131 — UC: Pit Wall gold accent

Created: 2026-10-29 · Status: planned

## Goal

Pit Wall, the analytics portal and the surfaces in the app that carry what it says, used the same
red as everything else in Undercut. Red reads as "bad" (it is also the colour of a negative number,
a fee and an error), so the premium product looked like a warning. Pit Wall's accent is now gold:
in the portal, a `--gold` token (with `--onGold` ink and a `--goldFaint` tint) replaces `--red` for
every accent use, and a `--neg` token keeps the old red for the things that really are bad (DNF
counts, losses, early-termination fees, a blown scroll budget, errors). In the app, the Grid theme
gains `gold` / `goldFaint`, and the Pit Wall pick band in the picker, the `PIT WALL · ACE CALL`
box in the driver sheet, the Pit Wall analysis block, the `PIT WALL` ace line on the Team screen,
the Pass row on the Profile and the in-app browser's controls use them; the rest of the app keeps
its red. The amber `warning` / `--warn` moves to orange so a caution is not the same colour as the
accent, and the portal's favicon and touch icons carry the gold sweep.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `web/pitwall/src/styles.css` has no `--red` / `--onRed` / `--redFaint` token; every former accent use reads `var(--gold)` or `var(--onGold)` (`--goldFaint` is declared for parity with the app and has no portal use yet); `.err`, `.neg` and `.pill.n` / `.meter.n` read `var(--neg)`, and the light `--neg` is `#C40F15` (the old `#E5161C` was 3.8:1 on cards).
- [ ] In the portal, `Pill` takes `gold` / `neg` and `Meter` takes `neg`; no component uses a `red` class or prop (`rg '\bred\b' web/pitwall/src` finds only comments, team names and test fixtures).
- [ ] Negative values stay red: price drops (`▼`), DNF counts, a negative gain, an early-termination fee, THREAT, a hit rate under 45 %, the scroll budget `over`, Moonshot errors.
- [ ] Cautions read `--warn` (orange), neither gold nor red: rain at or above 1 mm, the Hero's penalty-risk / open-slot flags, stale data in the context bar, unsaved changes in the Lineup Lab, Moonshot's "Double down" and "terms changed" notes.
- [ ] `S_COLORS_DARK` / `S_COLORS_LIGHT` carry `gold` and `goldFaint`; the dark `warning` is `#FF8A1F`; `valuePillContrast.test.ts` still passes.
- [ ] The picker's Pit Wall pick band (`goldFaint` tint, `gold` edge bar and `PIT WALL PICK` label), the ace-call box, `GridPitWallBlock`, the Team screen's `PIT WALL` line and the Profile's Pit Wall rows use `colors.gold`; `colors.primary` is untouched everywhere else.
- [ ] Gold meets WCAG AA for normal text on the card surface in both themes: dark ≥ 9:1 (`#D3BC8D` is 9.4:1 on the app's `#1A1A1A`, 9.8:1 on the portal's `#161616`), light ≥ 5:1 (`#73602F` is 5.1:1 on `#E8E8E5`).
- [ ] Portal preview screenshots (dark + light, desktop + phone) and an emulator run of the app's picker and driver sheet show gold where red was, filed on the share under `/mnt/smb/share/undercut/enh/pitwall-gold/` (01–09, as for PR #238's evidence) and linked from the PR; `docs/design/pitwall/briefing/HANDOFF.md` records the new tokens.
- [ ] No forbidden term is introduced (the tokens and copy name no series or sanctioning body).
