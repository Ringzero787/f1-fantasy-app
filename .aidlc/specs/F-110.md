# F-110 — UC: Moonshot follows the car when a substitute drives, and the enable script

Created: 2026-10-07 · Status: building · Design source: the owner's decisions of 2026-10-07 on F-106–F-109 (`docs/design/moonshot/README.md`)

## Goal
Undercut (root app). Two things the owner decided once the Moonshot features were deployed. First, the settlement rule for a driver who does not take part: "all drivers race — if there is a sub you pick the car that driver was in and assume the new driver in that car; DNS and DNF mean no points (you lose)". So a call follows the car to a substitute, and an absent driver with no substitute costs the stake. Second, the switch itself: a script that writes the owner's `config/app.moonshot` block through a `uc-script` op — on, priced continuously, DNS and DNF as losses, live timing off — so the mechanic opens without a build.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **The call follows the car.** `resolveSeat(driverId, results, drivers)` (pure, `functions/src/moonshot/substitute.ts`): the called driver's own result when present; otherwise the one row in the classification for the called driver's constructor whose driver is not one of that constructor's regular (active) drivers — the substitute. A regular team-mate is never the substitute; with no such row, or more than one, the driver counts as absent. Settlement uses the resolved result for the decision and records `settledOnDriverId` on the call and the feed entry. Tests: substitute found, team-mate ignored, nobody in the seat, unknown driver, two strangers, and the substitute's podium settling the caller's hit.
- [ ] **DNS and DNF lose.** With the owner's block (`dnsRule: LOSS`, `dnfRule: LOSS`), an absent driver without a substitute, a DNS and a DNF all settle as MISSED (test). `nc` already follows the DNF rule (F-107). Cancelled race stays VOID (no race was run).
- [ ] **The enable script.** `functions/scripts/setMoonshotConfig.js` follows the op-script convention (`scripts/seedGuards.test.js`: nothing on import, `initAdmin` on `SA_KEY` with the project check, `--apply` to write): a dry run prints the live block beside the desired one; `--apply` merges `enabled: true`, `unlockRound: 13`, `pricing.mode: continuous`, `dnsRule/dnfRule/dsqRule: LOSS`, `cancelledRule: VOID`, `liveTiming: false` into `config/app.moonshot` at the field level, so bands, stake levels and copy set elsewhere survive. Flags `--enabled`, `--mode`, `--liveTiming`, `--unlockRound` adjust it; an unknown flag refuses. Runs through `aidlc op new uc-script -p script=setMoonshotConfig.js -p backup=config/app`.
- [ ] The design document reads "Undercut" where it said "Regal Leagues" (the owner's misstatement, confirmed 2026-10-07).

## Data
No new collections. `moonshots/{id}.settledOnDriverId` and `leagues/{id}/activity/{id}.settledOnDriverId` record which driver's result a call settled on. `config/app.moonshot` gains `liveTiming` (already read by the app, F-108) and the owner's rule values.

## Performance budget
