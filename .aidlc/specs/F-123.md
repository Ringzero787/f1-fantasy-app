# F-123 — UC: Marketing site and store listings

Created: 2026-10-14 · Status: planned · Owner 2026-10-14 (closed-beta pack)

## Goal
Undercut's public face. `public/index.html` still leads with an APK download, the name
“The Undercut”, and “Fantasy Formula 1”. A commissioner sending a join code needs store badges,
three screenshots, and the product in one sentence: private league, contracts, Ace, join-by-code.
Play and App Store name lines still carry “Formula 1”; that word stays in the body only.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **`public/index.html`** is titled **Undercut** (not “The Undercut”). The hero is store
      badges (App Store and Google Play), not an APK. Body names private leagues, contracts, Ace,
      and join-by-code. Three screenshots. Join-by-code points at `/join`. Formula 1 may appear in
      descriptive body copy; it does not appear in the document title or the H1.
- [ ] **`docs/store/play-listing.md`** and **`docs/store/app-store-listing.md`** hold the short
      description. The name / title / subtitle line has no watchlist term (F1, Formula 1, FIA,
      Grand Prix). The body may describe fantasy motorsport over a Formula 1 season.
- [ ] Amazon listing (`docs/store/amazon-listing.md`) already matches; leave it unless a field
      drifts.
- [ ] A unit test reads the site and the listing files and fails if the name line contains a
      watchlist term or the hero still links `/undercut.apk`.
- [ ] Hosting deploy is an `aidlc op` (`hosting-deploy`) after this lands. Play en-US listing is
      published via `uc-play-listing` from `docs/store/play-listing.md`. App Store Connect is
      still pasted by hand from `docs/store/app-store-listing.md`.

## Targets
- web (`public/`, Firebase Hosting)
- android / ios store metadata (docs only; the portals are the live listing)

## Data
No user data. Screenshots are product UI, not a live league.

## Performance budget
Static page. Three PNG screenshots, each under 600 KB.

## Out of scope
- Rewriting privacy/terms (descriptive “Formula 1” in legal pages stays).
- Deleting `public/undercut.apk` from Hosting.
- Track Limits consumer site.
- Paid ads.

## Operations
- **hosting-deploy** for `public/` and `firebase.json`.
- **uc-play-listing** for the Play en-US title, short description and full description.
