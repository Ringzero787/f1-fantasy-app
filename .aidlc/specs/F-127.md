# F-127 — UC: Moonshot in the weekend recap

Created: 2026-10-25 · Status: implemented, unreleased · Reported from the field by the owner after
the United States round, 2026-10-25: "my moonshot didn't show up in my weekly summary. just verst
doing well in points. it was kind of a sad summary actually. which needs to be fixed as well."

## Goal
Undercut (app only; no server, rules or portal change). The WEEKEND RECAP is the first thing a
player sees after a race is scored, and until now it was the roster alone: the points, the top and
lowest scorer, the six lines. The team's Moonshot — the one call of the weekend that was theirs
and not the roster's — was never on it, even when it had just cost them 200 points. The server
settles the call and writes everything needed onto the team's own call document before the recap
can appear (F-107); the recap simply never read it. It now does: a Moonshot card between the
weekend's points and the scorers, and a line that adds the two up.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **One pure rule for which call the recap shows** (`recapMoonshot` in
      `src/simple/grid/moonshot.ts`): this team's call on the recap's race, once it has a result
      (HIT, MISSED or VOID). A call still open or locked has nothing to recap yet, a cancelled one
      never counted, and a second team's call is the second team's (tokens are per team). Tests in
      `__tests__/simple/recapMoonshot.test.ts`.
- [x] **The card's words are pure and tested** (`recapMoonshotLines`): the state word from
      `MOONSHOT_COPY` (`stateHit` / `stateMissed` / `stateVoid`, server-overridable through
      `config/app.moonshot.copy` and covered by the forbidden-terms rule), `DRIVER — WIN` through
      `predictionLabel`, `FINISHED P3` or `NOT CLASSIFIED` (a void shows the existing
      `resultVoid` line, token returned), the signed adjustment with its currency, a one-sentence
      spoken form for a screen reader, and the total line: `ROSTER +283 · MOONSHOT −200 ·
      WEEKEND +83` for a points stake, `ROSTER +283 · MOONSHOT −100 CASH` for a cash stake (cash is
      never added to points), nothing for a void.
- [x] **The recap loads the calls itself.** `useWeekendRecap` asks the Moonshot store for the
      team's calls on the race's season when a recap is due (Moonshot enabled, signed in, a team,
      the race's season known, the recap not yet seen); the store answers one read per team and
      season, shared with the Team screen, so a recap shown before the Team screen asked costs
      nothing extra and shows the call. Off, the recap carries no Moonshot and reads nothing.
- [x] **The card sits between the weekend's points and TOP SCORER / LOWEST**: `🚀 MOONSHOT`, the
      state word on the right, the call in the display face, the finish on the left and the signed
      amount on the right, bordered in the outcome's colour (positive for a hit, primary for a
      miss, muted for a void, as the Team screen's card). Rows wrap rather than clip. The card is
      one accessible element whose label is the spoken sentence.
      (`docs/design/grid/verify/F-127-web-recap-moonshot-missed.png`, `-hit.png`, `-void.png`,
      `-missed-light.png`.)
- [x] **The hero number stays the roster's points**, which is what the league table ranks on
      (`points` on the leaderboard entry; the Moonshot is its own column). The total line under the
      scorers is where the two meet, and it appears only when a Moonshot settled on the race.
- [x] **The round report (F-120) is untouched.** It already lists every Moonshot settled on the
      race from the league's activity; the recap's card is the player's own, from their own call
      document, so the two never disagree on the number: both read the server's
      `adjustmentAmount`.
- [x] Typecheck adds no errors, eslint is clean on the changed files, and the Moonshot, round
      report and new recap tests pass (73 tests across four suites).

## Targets
- android
- ios

## Data
No user data. The recap reads the team's own Moonshot documents, which the Team screen already
reads under the same rules; nothing new is stored or sent.

## Performance budget
At most one extra Firestore read per team per season, and none when the Team screen already made
it. Bundle +2 KB source.

## Out of scope
- The round report's content and the share card (F-120).
- A per-driver Moonshot line inside the entity list (the card above it is the Moonshot).
- Verified on Expo web in demo mode, both themes, through a throwaway forced path (the recap does
  not appear in demo mode: `scoredRaces` never includes the race) that fed the real card a settled
  call for a miss, a hit and a void; the throwaway was reverted before the commit. Not driven on a
  device build. The hit's total line wraps to two lines at 396 px with a four-digit sum, which is
  the intended behaviour at XXL too (wrap, never clip); not repeated at XXL on a device.
- Shipping: the next store build after 2.6.0 (`.aidlc/releases/`).
