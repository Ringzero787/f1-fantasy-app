# F-135 — UC: Pit Wall check flags a race held by results ingestion

Created: 2026-11-02 · Status: planned

## Goal

The results ingestion parks a race in `pendingResults/{raceId}` (status `pending`, with a
`blockedReason` when the provider's grid has a car it cannot map) and then skips it on every tick,
and nothing tells anyone. Mexico City 2026 sat unscored from 23:00Z to 02:00Z that way until the
owner noticed (F-134). The twice-weekly check (F-132) already reads the registry and the worker;
it now reads the held results too, so a parked race is a FAIL with the reason and the first action
in the report the owner gets.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `scripts/ops/pitwall-check.js` reads `pendingResults` where `status == 'pending'` into
      `state.held` (`raceId`, `round`, `fetchedAt`, one-line `blockedReason` ≤ 200 chars).
- [ ] `evaluate`: a held result with a `blockedReason` is FAIL under `scoring`, naming the race,
      how long ago it was fetched, the reason and the fix path (mapping → deploy the checkers →
      `requeueBlockedResults` op); one without a reason is WARN (awaiting admin approval).
- [ ] A live race more than three hours past its start with nothing held is WARN ("nothing fetched
      yet"); more than twelve hours past stays FAIL as before; under three hours is quiet.
- [ ] In the 36 h after the previous round's race, a portal payload one round behind the calendar
      is a WARN naming the turnover (the worker publishes the new round at its next 06:00Z job),
      not the FAIL that means a hang; after 36 h it is the FAIL as before.
- [ ] Tests cover held-with-reason, held-without-reason, the 3 h WARN, the 12 h FAIL, the quiet
      case and the healthy fixture; the suite passes.

## Targets
- binary (ops script on the build box, cron Mon + Thu)

## Data
No user data. Reads `pendingResults` with the same service account the check already uses.

## Performance budget
One extra Firestore read (normally zero documents).

## Out of scope
- Alerting from the ingestion itself (an email or pw_alert when it parks a race) — worth doing,
  but it lives in Cloud Functions and needs a deploy; this closes the gap from the check side first.
