# F-130 — UC: Weekend recap from the round record

Created: 2026-10-27 · Status: planned

## Goal

The post-weekend recap (the card that opens once after a race the team was scored for) scored the
roster as it stands today against the race's neutral points, so a driver sold after the race left
"your" weekend, a one-driver roster read as the same number three times, and nothing said where the
weekend left the player or what was coming. The scoring run already records the weekend per team
(`fantasyTeams/{teamId}/raceSnapshots/{raceId}`, F-029: the roster as fielded, the Ace, auto-filled
picks, every phase's points per pick, the stale-roster penalty) and per league
(`leagues/{id}/raceResults/{raceId}`, F-062). The recap now reads those two documents and says:
what the team was credited, pick by pick as scored; what was fielded and what was not; the place in
the round and the gap to its winner; the season rank and how it moved; the team's settled Moonshot
(F-127, unchanged); and the next round with its lock and the team's Moonshot on it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The hero number is the sum of the snapshot's phases (qualifying, sprint, race), including the stale-roster adjustment; when no snapshot exists the card falls back to the roster as it is now and says so (`FALLBACK_NOTE`).
- [ ] Each pick row shows the points the server credited for that pick across all phases, with `ACE ×2`, `TEAM` and `AUTO` badges from the snapshot; picks that scored 0 collapse into one line.
- [ ] A lineup with open slots at scoring shows `FIELDED n OF 6 · m OPEN SLOTS SCORED NOTHING`; a stale-roster penalty shows as its own line.
- [ ] In a league, the card shows the round rank and gap to the round's winner from the leaderboard the server wrote (`ROUND WINNER · +N` for a winner) and the season rank with ▲/▼ movement from the member list.
- [ ] The card ends with `NEXT · RD n · CITY`, the lock countdown, and the team's Moonshot on that round (`IN PLAY` / `LOCKED` with the call, or how many shots are left); nothing about Moonshot when it is off or not yet unlocked.
- [ ] The recap waits for the record reads and for the Moonshot config to load before showing (a dismissed recap never returns; a card shown before the config loaded lost the player's Moonshot) and never reads the record in demo mode; a failed read falls back rather than hiding the recap; a missing leaderboard is asked for once more after a short wait.
- [ ] The card names the team it is about and, for a two-team player, shows `TEAM n / 2`; "seen" is kept per team, so each team gets its own recap, and the card offers `NEXT · <other team>` when the other team's recap for the race is still unseen.
- [ ] The next-round Moonshot line says nothing when the season's calls could not be read.
- [ ] The race title drops the series' own words (`shortRaceName`); no line uses a forbidden term (pinned in `__tests__/simple/weekendRecap.test.ts`).
- [ ] The F-127 Moonshot card and the F-120 LEAGUE SCORES step are unchanged (their pinned tests still pass).

## Targets
- android
- ios
- web

## Data
No new user data. Two additional client reads per recap: the team's own race snapshot (readable by its owner and league under the existing F-029 rule) and the league's race leaderboard (existing F-062 rule, already read by the round report). Nothing is written.

## Performance budget
Bundle +~3 KB (one pure module). Two one-document reads once per team and race, both cached for the session; the recap is held until they answer (typically well under a second), which is already the case for the Moonshot read.

## Out of scope
- Localising the recap's lines (the round report's are English-only too).
- A recap for a team scored before F-029 existed (it gets the fallback).
- Changing what the server records.
