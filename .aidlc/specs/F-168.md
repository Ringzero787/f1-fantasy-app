# F-168 — UC: post-race result corrections re-grade teams

Created: 2026-11-22 · Status: building

## Goal
At Las Vegas 2026 the race scored at 06:25 UTC with Hadjar 14th and Lawson 15th. A time penalty then put Lawson ahead, but no Undercut team was corrected, and 90 teams kept 2–5 points they had not earned. There were two reasons:
- The correction pass only looked for 6 h from the race start.
- Race scoring skips every team already scored, so even a correction inside the window reached only Track Limits.

The same audit found that contract expiry sold expired picks at pre-race prices and reverted the refreshed prices on the rest of the roster. This must not depend on someone noticing: a correction should be applied by itself every time, and a missed one should page.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `onRaceCompleted`: when a completed race's results change, the difference is applied to each team that fielded the race (`applyRaceCorrection`):
  - Each pick moves by (new − old neutral points) × the Ace factor, per component (race, qualifying, sprint), into the phase where that component was scored.
  - The Ace counts only if the pick was under the 200 cap at its pre-race price (priceHistory `previousPrice`), the same rule as scoring.
  - Picks still held on the same contract move `pointsScored` and `totalPoints`. Picks sold, expired or re-bought since move `lockedPoints`.
  - The race snapshot, `raceScores`, best race and league standings (`syncLeaguesForRace`, extracted from Phase 4) are rewritten.
  - Prices are not rewound.
  - (raceCorrection.test.js)
- [ ] Each team is corrected once per results version: in a transaction, keyed by `resultsSig` on its snapshot, and the snapshot keeps a `corrections` audit trail. A correction to a race older than the season's latest scored race is refused. Settled Moonshots on a moved driver are listed for review, not re-settled.
- [ ] The race-score breakdown is one pure function, `buildRaceScoreRows`, used by both scoring and correction. Rebuilt from the stored results of five 2026 races, it reproduces 164 of 165 stored rows. The odd one is a stored result with no grid position, where old and new code compute the same expression.
- [ ] The ingestion's correction pass re-fetches a completed race for 72 h after the race, up from 6 h. The fetch step is shared as `fetchOfficialResults`.
- [ ] Contract expiry (Phase 3.5) sells at the post-race price and keeps the refreshed prices on the remaining roster. Negative banked points are kept (`lockedPoints !== 0`).
- [ ] The ops check compares races completed in the last 7 days with the provider's classification: a WARN inside 72 h, a FAIL after it, naming the repair op (pitwall-check.test.js).
- [ ] Repairs, as `uc-script` ops that dry-run by default:
  - `correctRaceResults.js` corrects the latest completed race. For Las Vegas 2026 the dry run gives 90 teams changed (47 −2, 11 −3, 32 −5) plus 3 teams with offsetting picks, 4 rows and 19 leagues, matching an independent recount.
  - `repairExpirySale.js` re-credits expiry sales at the post-race price. For Las Vegas 2026: 2 teams change (−53, +47) and 4 teams get their roster prices refreshed.

## Targets
- Undercut functions: `onRaceCompleted`, `checkOpenF1Results`. Deploy via the `functions-deploy` op, which the owner applies.
- Ops box: `scripts/ops/pitwall-check*.js`. `/data/f1-app-openf1` moves to the merge commit.
- No app or rules change.

## Data
Team scores, budgets and league standings are changed by the repair ops and by future corrections. No personal data. Writing corrected results to `races/{id}` also makes Track Limits re-grade that race from the same document, which is the intended shared behaviour.

## Performance budget
A correction reads every team and its snapshot for the race, about 100 of each, and runs one transaction per team: a few seconds, once per correction.

## Out of scope
- Rewinding prices after a correction.
- Re-settling Moonshots: they are listed for review.
- The stale-roster penalty on auto-filled teams, which is an open owner decision.
