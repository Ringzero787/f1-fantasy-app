# F-133 — UC: Pit Wall worker claim query starved by finished jobs (F-133)

Created: 2026-10-29 · Status: planned

## Goal

Pit Wall readers get the day's projections and briefing at 06:00Z without anyone running an op. On
2026-10-29 the twice-weekly check (F-132) found the worker ticking every five minutes with a fresh
heartbeat while today's `projections__2026__21__daily-20261029` and `briefing__…` sat `queued`, due
since 06:00Z, with zero attempts. The claim query in `FirestoreJobStore` asked for jobs with
`notBefore <= now`, oldest first, limit 25: once twenty-five finished jobs lay in the past (which
happened on 2026-10-07) the window held nothing but history and the loop skipped every one of them.
The 2026-10-11 hang (F-132) masked it. This fixes the query so the worker claims due work however
much history the queue carries, and teaches the check to see a due job nobody claims.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `FirestoreJobStore.claim` reads only open jobs (`status in queued|leased|failed`), applies the
      lease rules in memory and takes the earliest claimable one by `notBefore` then id, the same
      order as `MemoryJobStore`; it needs no composite index.
- [ ] A unit test puts thirty finished jobs ahead of one due queued job in a fake Firestore and
      asserts the queued job is claimed; the same fake with no claimable job returns null.
- [ ] A job claimed more than 24 h after `notBefore` (`lease.STALE_AFTER_MS`) is marked `skipped`
      with the reason and never run or retried: after an outage the backlog (nineteen jobs for
      rounds 18–19 on 2026-10-29) must not spend model tokens on briefings for raced rounds or
      write session payloads days late. A job hours late still runs. Unit tests cover both and the
      runner without the option behaves as before.
- [ ] `scripts/ops/pitwall-check.js` reads the open queue and `evaluate` adds a `queue` check:
      a `queued` job more than an hour past `notBefore` is FAIL, a `leased` job whose lease ran out
      more than an hour ago is FAIL, a `failed` job at the attempt limit is WARN; tests cover each
      and the healthy fixture stays OK.
- [ ] The worker suite (50 tests) and the check suite (12) pass; `node --test` exits 0, nothing cancelled.
