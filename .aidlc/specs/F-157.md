# F-157 — UC: app reads the current season everywhere (2027-ready build)

Created: 2026-11-17 · Status: planned

## Goal

The app still carries the year. `src/simple/grid/GridLeagueManager.tsx` creates leagues with a literal `'2026'`; 33 lines under `src/` and `app/` hold the year as a literal; the last-race view takes the latest round across all seasons, so in 2027 it would sit on Abu Dhabi 2026; and the client's race clock can disagree with the server's season. The server side is already season-aware (F-149, F-151); this is the app half, and it needs a store build on all three stores before the opener.

Source: the 2026-11-17 EGM review of 2027 readiness (finding R-03); the evidence lines were re-checked against `origin/master` on that date. Dates are proposals worked back from the 2027-03-14 opener, not owner decisions, unless marked.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] No year literal decides behaviour in `src/` or `app/`: the season comes from `config/app.currentSeason`, with a test that fails on a new literal.
- [ ] `raceScores` queries and the last-race view are scoped to the current season.
- [ ] League creation stamps the current season.
- [ ] Run against a rolled-over copy (F-154): Team, Market, League, recap and round report show 2027 state, and 2026 history is still reachable where the design keeps it.
- [ ] Released on Play, the App Store and Amazon; the live version per store is recorded.
