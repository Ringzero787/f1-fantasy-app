# F-095 — UC: the ace lock holds on the server, not only in the app

Created: 2026-10-03 · Built: 2026-10-04 · Status: building

## The gap
Undercut (root app, the Pit Wall portal, the Firestore rules and the lock sweep).

The ace doubles a driver's or constructor's points. It locks at **race start** — later than the
roster, which locks at qualifying — and that later lock existed **only in the client**:

| | locks when | enforced by |
|---|---|---|
| roster (drivers, constructor, budget) | qualifying | `isLocked`, the rules' denied-field list, the team callables |
| **ace** | **race start** | **`lockout.ts:102` in the app, and nothing else** |

- `firestore.rules` denied a named list of fields. **`aceDriverId` and `aceConstructorId` were not
  on it**, and `isLocked` was protected *from being changed* rather than consulted as a condition.
- `web/pitwall/src/lib/teamApi.ts` writes the ace with a direct `updateDoc`; the app writes it
  through `syncTeamToFirebase` after an equally client-side check.
- `functions/src/scoring/calculatePoints.ts:318` reads `team.aceDriverId` **live** when it scores,
  and re-checks only `ACE_MAX_PRICE`. It never asked whether the race had started.

**So: between lights out and the moment scoring ran, a player could set their ace to whoever was
winning and have those points doubled.** That window is hours wide and needs no exploit beyond the
Firebase web SDK and the player's own credentials.

## Why the obvious fix was wrong
A one-line rule denying ace changes while `isLocked == true` was written and reverted before it
reached production. It would have broken a designed feature: `isLocked` is the *qualifying* lock,
and moving the ace after seeing qualifying — right up to lights out — is deliberate.
`lockout.ts:101-102` says so in two lines:

```
isNaturallyLocked    = now >= lockTime        (qualifying)
isAceNaturallyLocked = now >= raceStartTime   (race start)
```

Any fix has to distinguish the two locks, and the rules cannot read a race start time out of the
race document without a `get()` the team doc gives them no key for.

## Platforms and data

**Platforms:** Firestore security rules; Cloud Functions (`autoLockTeams`, `autoUnlockTeams`,
`seasonLockTeam`, `earlyUnlockTeam`, `createTeamSecure`); the Expo app (Android, iOS, Amazon — the
same JS bundle on all three); the Pit Wall web portal. No native code, manifest, entitlement or
build flag is touched, so no store declaration changes.

**Data touched:** two new fields, **`fantasyTeams/<id>.lockStatus.aceLockTime`** and
**`.aceLockUntil`** — Timestamps equal to that weekend's `races/<id>.schedule.race` and that plus
the 24-hour failsafe ceiling the roster unlock already uses, or null. Written only by the lock
sweep and the unlock paths; readable by whoever can already read the team (its owner and their
league). They are race start times, which are public: no personal data, nothing of a player's own
in them, nothing needing retention or export treatment. Absent on every team written before this
change, and absence is the unlocked answer everywhere. No migration or backfill — the next lock
sweep stamps every team the weekend covers, and until then the rule reads exactly as it did
before.

## Acceptance criteria

1. With a team locked for qualifying and the race start still ahead, an owner can move the ace,
   clear it, or switch between a driver and a constructor ace — from the app and from the portal.
2. From the moment of race start until the team unlocks, every client-side write that changes
   `aceDriverId` or `aceConstructorId` is refused by Firestore, including one riding along with an
   otherwise permitted field.
3. Re-writing the ace it already has is not a change and is never refused — shipped clients send the
   whole metadata object on every sync.
4. Neither client offers an ace control it knows the server will refuse, and neither leaves a
   refused ace showing on screen as though it had saved.
5. No sequence of client calls — including `seasonLockTeam` then `earlyUnlockTeam` — clears or moves
   the deadline, or escapes the weekend lock while the race is running.
6. No team can end up with its ace permanently unchangeable: not a season-locked team, not one
   carrying a deadline from a race that is over, not one written before the field existed.
7. Teams and leagues created or stored before this change keep working unchanged, with no backfill.

## What was built
**The window is stamped on the team, so the rules can compare it to `request.time`.** No `get()`,
no second collection, and exact to the second rather than rounded up to the next sweep.

**It is a window, with an end, and that is the load-bearing decision.** The first attempt stamped
only a start and asked `isLocked == true` alongside it, so that a stale start could not freeze a
team for ever. Both model gates found the same consequence independently: anything that clears
`isLocked` then opens the ace. `seasonLockTeam` followed by `earlyUnlockTeam` did exactly that for
50 budget points, mid-race, and the `lockTeam` helper was an owner-callable that set `isLocked`
with nothing else — a lock the sweep would then skip, and so a team the deadline was never stamped
on at all. Giving the freeze an end removes the need to ask about the lock: a window cannot be
opened early by any write a client can make, and one nobody ever clears expires by itself.

1. **`functions/src/locks/teamLocks.ts`** — `autoLockTeams` already writes
   `lockStatus.nextUnlockTime` from `race.schedule.race` at the qualifying lock. It now also writes
   **`aceLockTime: race.schedule.race`** and **`aceLockUntil: race.schedule.race + 24h`**, and it
   stamps them on **every** team the weekend covers rather than only the ones it locks — its query
   dropped `where('isLocked', '==', false)`, because a team already locked when the sweep runs is
   one the rule would otherwise never see. The lock itself still only applies to teams not yet
   locked. `autoUnlockTeams` clears both (hygiene now, not correctness). `teamOperations.ts`
   creates teams with the fields present and null.
2. **`firestore.rules`** — a new `aceIsFrozen(team)` helper, and an update clause denying any diff
   touching `aceDriverId` or `aceConstructorId` while `request.time` is inside the window.
   `lockStatus` is already a denied key, so the window is not the client's to move. Half a window
   — a start with no end, or an end with no start — freezes nothing: fail open, not shut.
3. **`src/utils/lockout.ts`** — `serverAceLocked(lockStatus, now)`, mirroring the rule.
   The app's `GridTeamPanel` and all three store mutations (`setAce`, `setAceConstructor`,
   `clearAce`) take either it or the calendar-derived lock.
4. **`web/pitwall/src/data/team.ts`** — `aceFrozen(team, now)`, the same predicate, wired into the
   ace gate in `state.tsx` and the Compare card's button.
5. **`planSave` stops refusing an ace-only save.** It returned `blocked` on `isLocked` before
   looking at anything else, and every portal ace write goes through it — so without this, items 2
   and 4 above would have shipped as a change that did nothing from Saturday onwards. The lock now
   refuses a change to the *roster*, which is what it is for; an ace-only save has no steps.
6. **The three callables that could step around the lock.** The ace no longer depends on
   `isLocked`, but the *roster* still does, and the two should not come apart — the escape hatch
   was always both. `seasonLockTeam` and `earlyUnlockTeam` now refuse while a race weekend is
   live, and `lockTeam` is admin-only (nothing in the app, the portal or `scripts/` calls it).
   "Live" is read from the race calendar — `races` with `status == 'in_progress'`, which
   `autoLockTeams` sets at the qualifying lock and `checkResults` clears on completion — and not
   from the team's own fields. That distinction is the fix: a team season-locked *before* a
   weekend never receives a `nextUnlockTime`, so an earlier version that read one off the team
   answered "no weekend here" during exactly the race it was meant to protect.

### Two deliberate choices in that
**The rule only looks at the deadline while the team is locked.** Between races `aceLockTime` can
still hold the last race's start, which is in the past for ever; consulting it on an unlocked team
would freeze the ace permanently if any unlock path ever missed the clear. The clears are there as
well — stale state is how the next bug starts — but the rule does not depend on them.

**The Pit Wall portal gets *less* strict, not more.** It was gating the ace on `isLocked`, which is
the qualifying lock, so it refused every ace move from Saturday onward and threw away the window
the game deliberately gives you. It now refuses exactly when the server does. The Compare button
reads "The race has started" instead of "Locked for this weekend", and a bare `permission-denied`
from the direct ace write — the only step in `executePlan` that is not a callable, and so the only
one with no sentence of its own — is translated instead of surfacing as
"The server refused this step:".

## Why the scorer was left reading live
`calculatePoints` still reads `team.aceDriverId` at scoring time rather than from a frozen
snapshot. With the rules enforcing to the second there is no longer a window in which that value
can change after the race starts, and freezing the ace into `raceSnapshots` would mean reworking
the scoring path for a hole that is now closed. The sturdier version is still the better long-term
shape if any other field ever wants a race-start lock.

## Tests
- `rules-tests/firestore.rules.test.js` — five cases against the emulator: the quali-to-lights-out
  window still works; the ace is frozen after the deadline (driver, constructor, clear, and riding
  along with an innocent write); re-writing the *same* ace is not a change and is not blocked;
  a stale deadline on an unlocked team frees nothing; documents written before the field existed
  are unaffected; and the deadline itself cannot be moved by the owner. **Verified to fail against
  the previous rules file** — reverting `firestore.rules` fails exactly the freeze case.
- `__tests__/store/aceLockoutCalendar.test.ts` — three new cases, including the one that matters:
  five hours after the race the calendar has moved on to the next round and reports the ace free,
  while the team is still locked and still carries the deadline. **Verified to fail without the
  store guard.** Also the symmetric case: a stamp still in the future does not lock.
- The window's end is tested on all three surfaces: a stamp 72 hours old frees a season-locked
  team the unlock sweep never reaches (rules), and clearing `isLocked` mid-race frees nothing
  (rules, store, and both predicates).
- `web/pitwall/src/data/team.test.ts` — an ace-only save on a locked team is planned with no
  steps and no block, while a driver swap and a constructor swap on the same team are still
  refused. The pre-existing "blocks a locked team" case was asserting the bug and had to change.
- `__tests__/utils/lockout.test.ts` and `web/pitwall/src/data/team.test.ts` — the two predicates,
  including every shape the timestamp arrives in (Firestore `Timestamp`, the admin twin, the
  `{seconds}` object a persisted store rehydrates, ISO string, epoch ms) and the fail-open
  behaviour on an unreadable one.

## Deploy order
**Functions before rules.** The rule is inert until something stamps a deadline, so deploying it
first changes nothing; deploying functions first means the next lock sweep stamps teams that the
old rules do not yet consult, which is also harmless. Either order is safe — the pair only has to
be in place before the next qualifying. The app and portal changes ride their own releases; an
installed 2.4.2 client with no knowledge of the field simply sees its ace write refused and logs
it, which is the status quo for any denied write.

### Not covered by a test
There is no test harness for Cloud Functions in this repo — no `functions/**/*.test.ts` and no
runner in `functions/package.json` — so the `seasonLockTeam` / `earlyUnlockTeam` guards and the
sweep's stamp are covered by typecheck and review only. Building one is worth its own change; it is
the gap behind more than this feature.

## Still open
- **The ace also doubles sprint and qualifying points, which are scored before lights out.** The
  deadline stamped is `schedule.race`, matching what the app has always computed, so on the six
  sprint rounds a player can still move the ace during the sprint and have Saturday's points
  doubled by `checkSprintResults`. Closing it means locking the ace at the first session whose
  points it doubles, which shortens a window players can see and use today — a game-rule decision,
  not a bug fix, and one for the owner to make rather than something to slip into a security fix.
- **Leagues with `settings.lockDeadline: 'race'` are never locked at all.** The sweep only acts on
  `'qualifying'`. The type allows `'race'` (`src/types/index.ts:104`) but every creation path
  hardcodes `'qualifying'`, so no such league exists today. Pre-existing and wider than the ace.
- **A full recalculation re-scores past races with the *current* ace.**
  `recalculateAllTeamPoints` reads `team.aceDriverId` live for every historical round, outside any
  window this rule protects, so an admin repair run could retroactively double a pick set long
  afterwards. `raceSnapshots` already stores the ace per race and is the right source.
- **The portal has no ticking clock**, so a Compare button rendered before lights out stays enabled
  until something else re-renders. Clicking it is safe — `setAce` re-checks and says why — but it
  is a moment of the same offer-then-refuse the fix exists to remove.
- **A team created after the lock sweep is never locked for that weekend at all.** The sweep runs
  once, an hour before qualifying; a team made after that has `isLocked: false` through the race,
  so the roster callables (`teamOperations.ts:69` gates on `team.isLocked`) accept edits during
  the race and no ace window is stamped on it. Wider than the ace and not caused by this change:
  the roster is the bigger half. Self-penalising today — the player would have to abandon an
  existing team and its points — but it wants its own feature.
- **Nothing server-side enforces that the driver ace and the constructor ace are exclusive.** A
  direct write can set both and `calculatePoints` doubles both. Both clients clear the other when
  setting one, so no document should be in that state, and a rule would risk stranding one that
  already is. Pre-existing, and worth its own change with a look at the data first.
- The scorer still reads the ace live (above).

## Severity
A player could inflate their own score in a league others play honestly, and the Pit Wall Pass
makes that a paid product sitting next to an unfair ladder. No data was exposed and no other
account was reachable; the harm was competitive integrity.
