# F-136 — UC: post-results unlock visibility and shorter buffer

Created: 2026-11-03 · Status: planned

## Goal

After a race is scored the server keeps every team locked for a buffer and then a 30-minute
sweep unlocks it, while the app's calendar has already moved on to the next round four hours
after lights out. In that gap the Grid header said "LOCKS IN 5D" over dead rows with no
explanation (Mexico 2026: scored 02:00Z, reopened ~05:30Z, failsafe 20:00Z). Worse, the store
is local-first and skips the server on My Team focus once teams exist, so a copy taken during the
weekend kept `canModify: false` until a cold start, a pull-to-refresh or a roster write, none of
which a player staring at dead rows thinks to do. Three changes: say when the roster reopens,
re-read a locked team on focus, and cut the buffer from three hours to one.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `formatLockStatus` takes `serverLocked`, `serverUnlockAt`, `seasonLocked` and
      `resultsPending`; when the calendar is open and the server holds the team it returns
      `SEASON LOCKED` for a season lock, `RESULTS PENDING` while the stamp is still the
      pre-scoring failsafe, `REOPENS IN <countdown>` for a future stamp, `REOPENING SOON` for a
      past or missing one; a calendar lock still wins; an open team is unchanged.
- [ ] `serverRosterLock(team, now)` in `utils/lockout.ts` reads `lockStatus.canModify`,
      `nextUnlockTime` (Date, Firestore Timestamp, rehydrated `{seconds}` or ISO) and
      `isSeasonLocked` into that shape. A stamp more than two hours out is the lock sweep's
      race + 24 h failsafe, not a reopen time (scoring stamps completion + 1 h and the sweep adds
      up to 30 min), so it is reported as `resultsPending` with no countdown. Both Grid screens
      (Team panel, Picker) pass it to the status line.
- [ ] `useTeamStore.refreshTeamIfLocked()` re-reads the current team from the server only when
      the stored copy says `isLocked` or `canModify === false` and is not season-locked (no sweep
      lifts that), adopts the result into `currentTeam` and `userTeams`, never flips `isLoading`,
      is a no-op in demo mode, and logs rather than throws on failure. My Team's focus effect
      calls it when teams exist and one is selected.
- [ ] `onRaceCompleted` phase 5 stamps `nextUnlockTime` at completion + 1 hour (was 3); the
      comments that quoted the old buffer (calculatePoints.ts, teamLocks.ts, lockTime.ts,
      lockState.ts) say so.
- [ ] Tests: the six status cases, the lock helper's shapes, the past-stamp and failsafe-stamp
      cases, the store action's adopt / canModify-only / no-read / still-locked / failure cases;
      app typecheck, functions typecheck and both suites pass.

## Targets
- android
- ios
- binary (Cloud Functions: `onRaceCompleted`)

## Data
No user data beyond the team document the app already reads.

## Performance budget
One extra team read per My Team focus, only while the stored copy says locked by the weekend
(a season-locked team is skipped, so it does not pay a read every focus all season). Bundle
+0 KB of note.

## Out of scope
- A live Firestore listener on the team document (would cover every field, not just the lock;
  a bigger change to the local-first store).
- Changing the 30-minute unlock sweep cadence.
- The app half reaches players with the next store build; the server half ships on deploy.
- A render test of the two Grid screens' wiring (the status line is pure and tested; the
  screens pass one spread each). Verify on the next web-shot pass.
