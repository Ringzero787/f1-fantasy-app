# F-120 — UC: League round report after each race

Created: 2026-10-11 · Status: building · Asked for by the owner, 2026-10-11, with a screenshot of
another fantasy game's end-of-race screen: "clean, easy and shows who made the most progress …
this summary is for folks in a league that want to see how they compare. This could be a button
next to season under league, but also … a 'next' when you get the conclusion of the race."

## Goal
Undercut: the app, and one field added by the scoring run. After a race a manager already gets a
weekend recap of their own team (what each pick scored) and can look back at one race's league
table (F-062). What is missing is the short answer a league wants on Sunday night: who won the
round, who made the most progress, who had a bad one, and whose Moonshot landed. The round report
is that answer on one screen, in the Grid's own type and cards: your place, the podium, the
Moonshots of the round, then callouts in pairs. It opens from the League tab under the season
selector, and as the next step after the weekend recap.

Owner's decisions, 2026-10-11: include points per dollar ("efficiency"); keep the low score and
the biggest drop; make the Moonshots a feature of it, since the game this was modelled on has none.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **One rule set, in one pure module** (`src/simple/grid/roundReport.ts`, unit-tested): the
      report is built from the league's leaderboard for the race, the one for the round before
      (both written by F-062), and the Moonshots settled on the race (F-107's league activity).
- [x] **Your round first**, labelled YOUR ROUND: place of how many, race points, and the change
      against your own previous round when there is one to compare (tests). The recap before it
      labels its own figure SEASON, so the two places are not confused.
- [x] **Podium**: places one to three in rank order with points; a tie for a place stays on it,
      capped at five rows with the viewer never the one dropped (tests). A round nobody scored
      above zero in has no podium and no top score, as the server names no winner (tests).
- [x] **Moonshots of the round**: every call settled on this race, with who called it, the driver
      and the prediction, the model's chance, the multiplier and what it paid or cost. The **call
      of the round** is the hit the model gave the least chance, the bigger payout on level
      chances (tests). A call that followed the car is shown on the driver it settled on; a void
      pays nothing; a caller no longer on the leaderboard is shown unnamed; entries of a kind this
      build does not know are left out (tests). Nothing is shown when nobody called one.
- [x] **Biggest climb and biggest drop**: the largest gain and loss in race points against each
      manager's *own* previous round, named on the card ("MORE THAN RD 15"). Only managers scored
      in both rounds are compared; no climb when nobody gained, no drop when nobody lost; the
      first round on record has neither (tests).
- [x] **Top score and low score**: most and fewest race points. The low score is left out when
      everyone is level or fewer than three managers scored (tests).
- [x] **Best value and least value**: race points per $100 of the roster fielded, to one decimal.
      The scoring run now records each manager's roster value at lock on the leaderboard entry
      (`rosterValue`: the fielded roster at that weekend's prices, from the F-029 race snapshot;
      a manager's teams summed; null when it is not known for every one of their teams, never
      zero) (server tests). Only managers with a value are compared: two to name a best, three
      and a real gap to name a least; a leaderboard written before this says nothing (tests).
- [x] **Past rounds can be filled in** by `functions/scripts/backfillRaceResultRosterValue.js`,
      by the same rule, dry-run by default, changing only that field (server tests). Not run.
- [x] **Ties share a callout**, the viewer named first as YOU, three or more as "NAME & N OTHERS"
      (tests). A callout the viewer is in is outlined (not seen in a screenshot: the demo viewer
      is in none).
- [x] **Approximate data says so**: a leaderboard rebuilt from race-day points (F-062
      `estimated`) carries a matching RACE-DAY POINTS ONLY note (tests for the flag).
- [x] **From the League tab**: a ROUND REPORT row under the selector, for the race on screen or
      the latest race while the season table is showing; absent until the league has a
      leaderboard. It is its own row because the header row had no room for a third link
      (`F-120-ios-league-strip.png`, `F-120-ios-report-top.png`, `F-120-ios-report-bottom.png`).
- [x] **From the weekend recap**: anyone in a league gets NEXT · HOW THE LEAGUE DID with GOT IT
      beneath it; the report is the same card's second step, ending in DONE. It is not gated on
      the league's list of leaderboards, which refreshes in the background: a recap dismissed
      before that list landed would have lost the step for good. A league with no leaderboard for
      the race yet says so on the next step (`F-120-ios-recap-next.png`,
      `F-120-ios-recap-report-step.png`).
- [x] **Three reads at most** (two leaderboards and the race's Moonshots), cached for the
      session and shared with the League tab's own race table; cleared by pull-to-refresh and on
      sign-out, so the next account on the device is not served the last one's league.
- [x] **Large display sizes**: callouts stack one per row above size L and the League tab row
      wraps (`F-120-ios-xxl-league-strip.png`, `F-120-ios-xxl-report.png`).

## Targets
- android
- ios
- amazon

## Data
No new category of user data. The app reads `leagues/{id}/raceResults/{raceId}` for two races and
`leagues/{id}/activity` for one, both readable only by approved members and the owner, and both
already shown on the League tab (the race table and the Moonshot feed): display names, team
names, race points, and settled Moonshots. Nothing leaves the device.
**New on the server**: `rosterValue` on each leaderboard entry, a number derived from the roster
prices the race snapshot already holds. It shows league-mates what a manager's roster was worth
that weekend, which the app does not otherwise put a single figure on.

## Performance budget
Up to three reads when a report is opened (two documents and one small query), none otherwise;
the League tab's race table now shares the cache instead of reading the same document again.
Scoring: no extra reads (the snapshots were already loaded). No new dependency.

## Out of scope
- Movement in the season standings caused by the round (the season table already shows it).
- Sharing the report as text or an image.
- Avatars on the rows; the Grid league table does not use them either.
- A rule for a manager whose roster changed between the phases of one weekend: the snapshot keeps
  the roster of the last phase scored.

## Rollout
The app tolerates leaderboards with no roster value, so order does not matter for safety. To see
best and least value: deploy the scoring function (a `functions-deploy` op), and for rounds
already scored run the backfill (a `uc-script` op). Neither has been done.

## Verification
iOS simulator (Release build, demo data): the League tab row, the report top to bottom, both at
size M and XXL, and the recap's two steps. The recap does not appear in demo mode, so for its two
screenshots an uncommitted verification copy forced it to show; that proves the layout and the
hand-over between the steps, not when the recap itself appears. Server: unit tests and a clean
build; the scoring change has not run against real data and the backfill has not been dry-run.
Not checked: Android, a live league, dark theme, a screen reader, a league whose latest race has
no previous round, and the report on a leaderboard with no Moonshots and no roster values (covered
by tests only).
