# F-122 — UC: Picker arithmetic

Created: 2026-10-14 · Status: planned · Owner 2026-10-14 (closed-beta pack). Layers onto F-055
(Grid Pick Team). Distinct from F-044 (server auto-fill at lock).

## Goal
Undercut, the picker. New players and anyone filling empty seats after a sale currently do the
budget arithmetic in their head. The R16 audit found 37 of 40 teams on cheapest-first auto-fill.
This change puts **value** (season points ÷ price), **leftover budget for remaining slots**, and
two or three **pairing hints** on the picker so a person can finish a legal team without the app
building one for them.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Pure module** `src/simple/grid/pickerArithmetic.ts`, unit-tested, never writes a roster.
- [ ] **Value on every row.** `points ÷ price` over the season the client already holds; `null`
      when price is not positive. Shown on the row the way points and price already are, and
      available as a sort (`VAL`). Sorting is user-controlled; hints never reorder the list.
- [ ] **Leftover, forward.** While driver slots are empty: `LEAVES $X FOR N SLOTS` and the
      cheapest legal completion among unselected, unblocked drivers. Caution tone when the
      leftover cannot cover that completion.
- [ ] **Pairings after the first pick**, at most three, only on unselected unblocked rows, and
      gone once the driver list is full: `LEAVES $X FOR N` (adding this still leaves enough for
      the cheapest remaining slots), `TEAMMATE — SAME CAR`, `CHEAP + SCORES` (best value among
      the cheaper third). A hint is a badge; tapping the row is still the only way a pick moves.
- [ ] The same arithmetic covers a returning player filling seats after a sale (smaller budget,
      shorter list). No separate onboarding flow.
- [ ] `npx jest __tests__/simple/pickerArithmetic.test.ts` passes.

## Targets
- android
- ios
- amazon

## Data
No new user data. Computed on the device from prices, season points, constructor ids and the
pending lineup the picker already holds.

## Performance budget
O(n log n) on the ~20-driver market, once per pending change. No new payload, no new call.

## Out of scope
- Writing a roster or an accept-all suggested build (the earlier suggested-build was removed
  because it answers the question for the player).
- F-044 value auto-fill at lock (server, January).
- Modelled projections, which stay Pit Wall data; this feature is honest division of the past.
