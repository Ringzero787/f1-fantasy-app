# F-158 — UC: portal, worker and ops check follow the current season

Created: 2026-11-17 · Status: planned

## Goal

Pit Wall and its alarm are pinned to 2026. The portal has `SEASON = '2026'` (`web/pitwall/src/lib/env.ts`, and the same literal in `src/lib/account.ts` and `src/lib/teamApi.ts`), the worker defaults `PW_SEASON` to 2026 (`workers/pitwall/src/cli/serve.ts`, and `src/cli/project.ts`), and so does the ops check (`scripts/ops/pitwall-check.js`). In off-season mode the check reports OK until 75 days after the finale (about 2027-02-19), then only WARN, which sends no email, and it never fails for a 2027 race that nothing is published for. The projection model has no history for round 1 of a new season, and its circuit table (`workers/pitwall/src/model/circuits.ts`) lacks the two circuits new in 2027.

Source: the 2026-11-17 EGM review of 2027 readiness (finding R-04, R-18); the evidence lines were re-checked against `origin/master` on that date. Dates are proposals worked back from the 2027-03-14 opener, not owner decisions, unless marked.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The portal, the worker and the ops check read the season from `config/app.currentSeason`; no code change or env edit is needed at the next rollover, and a test fails on a new year literal.
- [ ] What each does when the field is absent or unreadable is specified and tested.
- [ ] Between the day the season switches and the day the 2027 grid is seeded (F-156), the worker finds a 2027 round with no 2027 lineup: the portal shows a stated pre-season state for that period instead of projections for last year's drivers.
- [ ] The ops check fails, not warns, when the current season has an upcoming race inside its window and nothing is published for it.
- [ ] What round-1 projections are built from is specified and implemented (previous-season form carried over, with what weight), and labelled as such on the portal.
- [ ] The circuit table covers every circuit on the 2027 calendar; a test compares it with the seeded races.
- [ ] Portal and worker deployed through their ops.
