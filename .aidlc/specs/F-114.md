# F-114 — UC: Pit Wall shows the league your team is in

Created: 2026-10-08 · Status: building · Design source: the owner, 2026-10-08 — "pitwall doesn't have a clean way to see the league you're in; we can add something to Lineup Lab"

## Goal
Undercut (Pit Wall portal). The portal never read the league a team plays in: the only league surface was one number from the worker's per-round `pw_leagues` document (`Rank n of N` in the Briefing hero), and that document exists only for rounds the worker has published. A player opening Pit Wall could not see who they are racing, where they stand or how the table moved. This adds the league to Lineup Lab as a standings tile, read from the same documents the app's league screen reads, and names the league in the context bar on every page.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **A League tile on Lineup Lab**, right column above the Moonshot tile: the league name; a pill with your position (`P3 of 10`, red when first) and your movement since the last race (▲/▼ from the server's `previousRank`; nothing for a member never ranked before); seats used of `maxMembers`; then the table — rank with movement, team and manager (and race wins where any), season total, last race (wide screens), Moonshot points — with your row marked the way every other "you" row in the portal is. Collapsed to the top five plus your own row when it sits lower; a `Show all N` chip expands it, so the scroll budget measures both states.
- [ ] **Order and rank are the server's.** `standingsRows` mirrors `orderMembers` (`functions/src/scoring/standingsFields.ts`: total, then last race, then id); rank is the position in that order, as the app's league screen shows it; a join awaiting approval and a withdrawn member are not in the table; a malformed member document still gets a row at the foot rather than breaking the page (tests).
- [ ] **Read directly, no worker, no callable.** `loadLeagueStandings(leagueId, uid)` reads `leagues/{id}`, `leagues/{id}/members` (ordered by `totalPoints`, limit 100) and `fantasyTeams where leagueId == X` (team names and `moonshotPoints`) — the three reads the rules allow a league member (the `fantasyTeams` list shape F-112 kept open for exactly this). A refusal or failure renders "could not be read", never a thrown render.
- [ ] **No request without a signed-in team.** The example payload — and so the preview build the scroll budget measures — renders a fixed ten-team example league from `exampleStandings()`; a signed-in team with no `leagueId` gets a pointer to the app. Two-team players switch league with the existing team chips (the tile keys on `real.team.leagueId`).
- [ ] **The context bar names the league** beside the team name on every page, from the `Account.leagueName` the sign-in already loaded (wide screens).
- [ ] Access key `league.standings` in the free set: the player's own league table is never behind the pass.

## Targets
- web (Pit Wall portal only; no app, functions or rules change)

## Data
No new documents. Three new client reads per Lineup Lab open per team: `leagues/{id}`, up to 100 member documents, up to 100 team documents.

## Performance budget
Bundle: the tile and the pure helper, well under the 150 KB gzip main-chunk budget. Scroll: Lineup Lab stays within 3.0 screens in every measured state (preview, bare, free), collapsed and expanded.

## Out of scope
- A standings page of its own, or standings on the Briefing (the hero's rank line stays as it is).
- Opening a rival's lineup from the table (the worker's `pw_leagues` lineup data is per round and not always published).
- The §30 Moonshot awards and a MOONSHOT column fed by the worker (deferred since F-109).
