# F-081 — UC: Global launch — five languages, store listings live for SEO, then open the countries

Created: 2026-09-25 · Status: planned

## Goal
Undercut (root app) and the Pit Wall portal go worldwide in two steps. Step one: as soon as the app reads well in German, French, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese and a Pit Wall Pass can be bought on every channel, the store listings go live in those languages so the pages start ranking ("baking for SEO") while distribution stays limited to today's countries. Step two, on the owner's say-so: open distribution to the other countries.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Gate A — language: the five launch languages (de, fr, es, it, pt-BR) are complete across every converted area of the app (F-079 parity gate G16 green for them) and read naturally on a device in each; the four others (nl, pl, ja, zh) may follow later and do not block.
- [ ] Gate B — money: a Pit Wall Pass can be bought and lands as the `pw` claim on all four channels — Google Play IAP, App Store IAP, Amazon IAP (F-060) and Stripe on the web (F-068) — each proven with a test purchase and a `purchases` document with `status: validated`.
- [ ] Step one — listings: Play, App Store and Amazon listings carry localised title, short and full description, screenshots and keywords for the five languages (store copy goes through the F-079 release-time translation, the trademark watchlist still applies to names); the listing is published so search engines and store search index it, while the country list stays as it is today.
- [ ] Step two — countries: on the owner's instruction, distribution is widened country by country (pricing templates reviewed per store, age ratings and data-safety declarations valid in each region); the change is recorded as an op and reversible.
- [ ] The Pit Wall portal follows the app's language (F-075 shell) at least for the paywall, the pass purchase and the Briefing chrome before step two.
- [ ] Support and privacy pages on humannpc.com exist in the five languages, or the listing links to English ones with a note.

## Targets

- web (portal)

No new personal data. Store data-safety and privacy declarations re-checked per region at step two.
