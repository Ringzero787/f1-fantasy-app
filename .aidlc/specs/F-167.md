# F-167 — UC: open lineup slot highlight

Created: 2026-11-22 · Status: building

## Goal
On the Team grid an open slot was a thin grey dashed outline on the page background, with a muted "+" and label. The owner said it "looks flat and almost like a filled slot". An open slot the player can fill should stand out softly, so a half-built lineup is obvious at a glance.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An open driver or constructor slot the player can fill has a soft accent wash (`primaryFaint`), a 1.5 px dashed accent border, the "+" in an accent ring, and an accent label. Checked in light and dark on Expo web (demo mode, 396×856).
- [ ] A locked or read-only open slot keeps the quiet look (grey dashed outline, muted text), because there is nothing to do with it, and reports `accessibilityState.disabled`.
- [ ] Filled tiles are unchanged.
- [ ] Sizes go through `scaled()`, so the ring grows with the display-size setting.

## Targets
- Undercut app: `src/simple/grid/GridTile.tsx`. Ships with the next store build. No functions, rules or data change.
