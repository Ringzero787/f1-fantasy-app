# F-167 — UC: open lineup slot highlight

Created: 2026-11-22 · Status: building

## Goal
On the Team grid an open slot was a thin grey dashed outline on the page background, with a muted "+" and label. The owner said it "looks flat and almost like a filled slot". An open slot the player can fill should stand out softly, so a half-built lineup is obvious at a glance.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] An open driver or constructor slot the player can fill has a soft accent wash (`primaryFaint`), a 1.5 px dashed accent border and the "+" in an accent ring (`openSlotLook`, openSlotLook.test.ts). Checked in light and dark on Expo web (demo mode, 396×856).
- [ ] Its small labels stay in the main text colour, at least 4.5:1 on the wash in both themes (tested). Red labels would be about 3:1 on the light wash.
- [ ] A locked or read-only open slot keeps the quiet look (grey dashed outline, muted text), because there is nothing to do with it, and reports `accessibilityState.disabled`.
- [ ] Filled tiles are unchanged.
- [ ] Sizes go through `scaled()`, so the ring grows with the display-size setting. Border strokes stay fixed hairlines, like every other tile border.

## Targets
- Undercut app on Android (Play and Amazon), iOS and web: `src/simple/grid/GridTile.tsx` and `src/simple/grid/openSlotLook.ts`. Ships with the next store build. No functions, rules or data change.
