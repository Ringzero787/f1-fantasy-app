# F-134 — UC: Results ingestion: stand-in driver with no team in the provider's race session (Mexico, Hirakawa)

Created: 2026-11-02 · Status: planned

## Goal

Mexico City 2026 (round 21) was fetched by `checkOpenF1Results` at 23:00:06Z on race day, on time
for the first time (F-128), and then held: Ryo Hirakawa drove the Haas in Ocon's place as car 50,
a number `DRIVER_NUMBER_TO_ID` does not know, so auto-approval was refused (the Zandvoort rule) and
the race sat at `pendingResults/mexico_2026` status `pending` while players waited for a score.
Mapping the number is not enough: the provider's Race-session driver record for car 50 has
`team_name: null` (its practice and qualifying records say "Haas F1 Team"), and a car without a
team is dropped by both converters with a "No team mapping" warning, so the Haas aggregate and the
Moonshot seat rule (F-110, the call follows the car) would still miss him. This maps car 50, lets
a car with no team in one session take the team it carried elsewhere in the meeting, and gives the
owner an op that re-queues a blocked result so the scheduler scores it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `DRIVER_NUMBER_TO_ID[50] === 'hirakawa'`, with no `drivers/` document (unpurchasable, unpriced,
      as Tsunoda at round 14); a test pins the mapping and that regular numbers are unchanged.
- [ ] `fillTeamsFromMeeting` (pure) fills a session driver with no team from the latest other
      session of the meeting that names a known team, appends one warning per fill naming the
      session and team, leaves unknown teams unresolved, never uses the session as its own
      fallback and never changes a car already mapped; tests cover each.
- [ ] Both the race and qualifying converters build their team map through `teamsForSession`,
      which makes the one extra `/drivers?meeting_key=` call only when a car lacks a team and
      degrades to a warning if that call fails.
- [ ] `functions/scripts/requeueBlockedResults.js` dry-runs by default, lists every `pending`
      result with a `blockedReason` whose race is not `completed`, and with `--apply` sets each
      to `rejected` (keeping the old reason under `requeuedFrom`) so `checkOpenF1Results`
      re-fetches, settles and approves it on its next tick; it refuses a key for another project
      and does nothing on import.
- [ ] `npm run build` and `node --test test/*.test.js` in `functions/` pass.

## Targets
- binary (Cloud Functions `checkOpenF1Results`, `checkQualifyingResults`, `checkSprintResults` share the converters)

## Data
No user data. Race classification and driver records from the provider, as before.

## Performance budget
One extra provider call per converter run only when a car has no team in the session.

## Out of scope
- A `drivers/hirakawa` document or price: he was a one-race stand-in by the information available.
- Ocon's own scoring: absent from the classification he is not re-priced and scores nothing, by
  the existing rule for drivers absent from the results.
- Scoring Mexico itself: that is the `uc-script` op running `requeueBlockedResults.js --apply`
  after the functions deploy, then the scheduler.
