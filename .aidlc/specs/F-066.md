# F-066 — UC: League Pro commissioner tools

Created: 2026-09-18 · Status: planned

## Goal
Undercut (root app), League Pro only. The person running an office or friends league gets the admin tools they currently handle in a spreadsheet: helpers, a league identity, and a ledger for the side pot. The app never moves money.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Owner can appoint up to 3 co-admins who can approve/remove members and edit league settings, but cannot delete the league, transfer ownership or buy/revoke Pro; enforced in rules (rules tests) and functions.
- [ ] League badge (from a built-in set) and accent colour are selectable and shown on the league header, standings and F-065 share cards.
- [ ] Side-pot ledger: commissioner records buy-in amount, who has paid and the payout split; members see it read-only. Entries are plain records; there is no payment, wallet, balance transfer or prize fulfilment in the app, and the screen says so.
- [ ] Store review check: listing and in-app copy describe the ledger as a tracking tool; no real-money gaming declaration is triggered (confirmed against current Play and App Store policy before release).
- [ ] All three tools are hidden with an upsell row when `isLeaguePro` is false; if Pro lapses next season, data is kept read-only.

## Targets
- firebase (rules, functions)
- android
- ios

## Data
Co-admin uids, league branding choice, ledger entries (amounts and paid flags entered by the commissioner) stored on the league; visible to league members only.
