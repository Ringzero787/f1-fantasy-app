# F-109 — UC: Moonshot in Pit Wall

Created: 2026-10-06 · Status: planned · Design source: `docs/design/moonshot/SPEC.md` §16, §21, §28–§30

## Goal
Undercut (root app), Pit Wall portal. The portal is where a manager studies a call before making it and follows it afterwards: every driver's Moonshot predictions priced from the same snapshot the app uses, the team's open and settled Moonshots on the Briefing, the league's declared Moonshots, and — for pass holders — the model view behind a multiplier. Placing a call from the web uses the same callables as the app.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Pricing is free.** The driver slide-over shows the Moonshot predictions with model chance, band and multiplier from `pw_moonshot_odds` for every signed-in user, because the price has to be visible to everyone. The pass adds the "why this multiplier" depth: the finishing-position distribution as a bar chart, the circuit and form splits behind it, calibration of the model's chance at this probability this season.
- [ ] **Call from the web**: the slide-over's Moonshot section runs the same currency → prediction → stake → confirm flow through `moonshotQuote` / `moonshotConfirm` / `moonshotCancel` (F-106), with the same two-tap minimum and the exact HIT/MISS numbers before confirm; the web never writes `moonshots`.
- [ ] **Briefing**: a "Your Moonshot" card when one is called for the round (prediction, chance, stake, HIT/MISS, state; Change/Cancel before lock), and tokens left; after settlement the result card. The lineup tiles mark a Moonshot driver with a 🚀.
- [ ] **League**: locked Moonshots of league-mates on the Briefing's rivals frame ("Turn One called Hadjar PODIUM · 200 pts · 5×"), private until lock.
- [ ] **Standings and recap**: the portal's standings show the `MOONSHOT` column and the expanded row of F-107; the Season page lists the season's Moonshot stats per team and the award candidates of SPEC §30 as they stand.
- [ ] Scroll budget and click-in rules of F-075 hold; terminology rule as F-108; locked (no-pass) treatment only on the depth view, never on the price.

## Targets
- web

## Data
Reads `moonshots` (own and league after lock), the snapshot and standings; writes only through the F-106 callables.

## Performance budget
Two reads per Briefing view (team's Moonshots, snapshot); one callable round-trip per quote.

## Out of scope
- Pricing, eligibility, settlement (F-106/F-107).
- The app experience (F-108).
