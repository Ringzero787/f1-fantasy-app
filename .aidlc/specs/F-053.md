# F-053 — UC: Grid sign-in and create-team reskin

Created: 2026-09-18 · Status: planned

## Goal

The login, register, forgot-password and first-run "Name your team" screens are reskinned with the Grid tokens so the first thing a new player sees matches the rest of the app: `surface` background, Unbounded 900 headline, mono captions, `card` inputs with 14 px radius, red primary pill button, provider pills for Google / Apple / Amazon. Layout and behaviour do not change.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `app/(auth)/login.tsx`, `register.tsx`, `forgot-password.tsx` render from `useSimpleTheme()` tokens (no `src/config/themes.ts` colours; the only literal colours are the providers' required button colours), in both dark and light — screenshots in `docs/design/grid/verify/F-053-*`.
- [ ] Social buttons keep their provider requirements on pills: Google light button with the G mark and "Continue with Google" (hidden on Amazon builds, as before), Apple black-on-light / white-on-dark per the HIG (iOS only), Amazon gold "Login with Amazon" on Amazon builds.
- [ ] `SimpleCreateTeam` becomes the one-field "Name your team" step in the League Manager form style (label, input, red CTA) and lands on the Team screen with 6 open slots; the optional league-code field stays.
- [ ] The `?demo=1` dev hook on login still enters demo mode.
- [ ] `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx jest --ci` pass; `npx expo export --platform android` succeeds.

No user data changes; auth providers unchanged.
