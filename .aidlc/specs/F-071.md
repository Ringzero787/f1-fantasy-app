# F-071 — UC: Pit Wall news wire and daily briefing

Created: 2026-09-19 · Status: planned

## Goal
Undercut (root app). The super fan wants to know what happened and what it means for their team in five minutes. Today `functions/src/news/fetchNews.ts` summarises two RSS feeds with Gemini into `articles`. The Wire widens the sources, clusters the same story across outlets, tags who it affects, and produces a daily briefing and a forward outlook per driver.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Ingest every 30 minutes from an allow-listed source table (official governing-body documents, team press releases, outlet RSS). Robots.txt and AI opt-outs are honoured; paywalled text is never fetched.
- [ ] Stories are de-duplicated into clusters; each cluster shows every source with attribution and a link out. Our text is at most two sentences, in our own words, stating the fantasy impact; no article structure, quotes or images are reproduced. (A 2026 ruling held that substitutive AI summaries can infringe; this is the safe pattern.)
- [ ] Each cluster is tagged with affected drivers/constructors, category (penalty, upgrade, reliability, weather, contract, steward decision), direction and magnitude; tags drive the impact chips on Briefing and each entity's slide-over.
- [ ] Daily briefing generated once per day (and after each session): "what changed since yesterday", five items max, each linked to its cluster and to the affected entity.
- [ ] Per-driver and per-constructor Outlook: past (season narrative from our stats), present (current form and news), future (next three rounds) written by an LLM from F-069/F-070 numbers and tagged stories only. Labelled "Estimate", shows the inputs it used and the generation time, and never states an unconfirmed penalty, injury or contract as fact.
- [ ] LLM provider sits behind one interface; default is Claude (`claude-sonnet-5` for briefings and outlooks, `claude-haiku-4-5` for tagging) with the key in Secret Manager. A validation pass rejects output that names an entity or number not present in the inputs.
- [ ] Admin review queue for anything tagged penalty or injury before it shows as confirmed.

## Data
Public news metadata and links. No personal data sent to the LLM.
