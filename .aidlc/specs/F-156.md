# F-156 — UC: 2027 car-number table, driver table season and lineup seed

Created: 2026-11-17 · Status: planned

## Goal

No 2027 race can be scored until this ships. `DRIVER_TABLE_SEASON` in `functions/src/ingestion/season.ts` is 2026, and a race from another season is refused at the door (`checkResults.ts`, "NOT INGESTING") rather than held, so the auto-retry of held results (F-152) never sees it and nothing alarms. The 2027 grid also has new drivers and moves.

Source: the 2026-11-17 EGM review of 2027 readiness (finding R-02); the evidence lines were re-checked against `origin/master` on that date. Dates are proposals worked back from the 2027-03-14 opener, not owner decisions, unless marked.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] `DRIVER_NUMBER_TO_ID` lists the 2027 car numbers from the provider's entry list (read it; do not infer numbers), and `DRIVER_TABLE_SEASON` is 2027.
- [ ] The 2027 drivers and constructors exist with the right seats. `setDriverSeat.js` retires one driver and adds one; it does not move a driver between teams and it refuses to retire a driver a team holds, so this needs either an extended script or a seeding op of its own, and it depends on F-155's market decision (a closed market means no team holds anyone).
- [ ] New drivers have opening prices by a stated rule (the rollover prices only drivers who scored in 2026).
- [ ] The full-season bonus follows the season's race count: `FULL_SEASON_RACES` is 23 in `functions/src/scoring/scoringCore.ts` and in `src/config/constants.ts`, and 2027 has 24 races.
- [ ] A 2027 race that is due while the table is still 2026's is **held with a reason**, not dropped, so the ops check and the auto-retry both see it; a test pins this.
- [ ] The Track Limits side is told before the deploy: it reads `drivers` and `constructors`.
- [ ] Functions deployed through an op before the first 2027 session.
