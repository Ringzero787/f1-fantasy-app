# F-173 — UC: correct a past race after the season

Created: 2026-11-24 · Status: planned

## Goal
The F-168 correction only fixes the latest scored race. It refuses an older one, because `lastRacePoints` and the weekend leaderboard belong to the newest race. F-172 found real post-race penalties at Mexico R21 (Piastri, Colapinto, Gasly) and Azerbaijan R17 (Bortoleto), which were scored on the provisional order. Owner decision, 2026-11-24: correct both **after the 2026 season**. The impact measured read-only that day was 30 teams and 177 points of absolute change. No league rank or global rank moves; the largest single change is G9IrVx −44, because Gasly was its ×2 ace.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The penalties are confirmed against the FIA decision documents for both races before any apply. The provider does not publish the stewards' decisions.
- [ ] Only classification is corrected: the provider's position and status. The stored grid, laps, fastest lap, qualifying and sprint are kept, because the provider's `/starting_grid` now returns 404 and the grid would fall back to qualifying.
- [ ] `races/{id}.results` is **not** written for a past race. Track Limits' `tlOnRaceCompleted` would re-grade lineups and bets with today's tiers. The corrected classification is kept in a separate record with an audit stamp.
- [ ] It may touch: team `totalPoints`/`lockedPoints`/pick `pointsScored`, the race's snapshots, its `raceScores` rows, best race, league member totals and `rank` (ranked without a raceId, so `previousRank` and the arrows are left alone), and that race's weekend leaderboard with `syncRaceWins`.
- [ ] It must not touch: `lastRacePoints`/`lastRaceId`, prices, `priceHistory`, Moonshot settlements (listed for review only), or `syncLeaguesForRace`.
- [ ] It refuses a race with no snapshots, a past season, and a run during a race weekend.
- [ ] A dry run and an emulator test (F-171 harness), then a uc-script op per race. After the apply, the race is removed from `ACKNOWLEDGED_DRIFT` (F-172).

## Targets
- Undercut functions (a script, `raceCorrection.ts` in a past-race mode). Ops: one uc-script op per race.

## Data
Team scores and league standings. No personal data.

## Out of scope
- Monaco R8, Britain R11, Australia R1, China R2 and Canada R7 (see F-172).

## Dates and decisions
- Owner decision 2026-11-24: after the season (after Abu Dhabi is scored), before or at the rollover.
