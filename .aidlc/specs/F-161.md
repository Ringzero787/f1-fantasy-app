# F-161 — UC: Expo SDK upgrade to clear the dependency waivers expiring 2027-03-01

Created: 2026-11-17 · Status: planned

## Goal

Four version-pinned dependency waivers in `.aidlc/waivers.yaml` expire on 2027-03-01, two weeks before the opener. From that day the security gate blocks every release unless they are resolved. F-148's notes say the Expo SDK upgrade is what clears them.

Source: the 2026-11-17 EGM review of 2027 readiness (finding R-11); the evidence lines were re-checked against `origin/master` on that date. Dates are proposals worked back from the 2027-03-14 opener, not owner decisions, unless marked.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Owner decision recorded:** do the SDK upgrade in the pre-season build (F-157), or extend the four waivers past the opener on purpose with a new date and reason.
- [ ] If upgrading: the app builds for all three stores, the launch is proven by running a Release build on a device or emulator (not by reading code), and the four waivers are removed.
- [ ] If extending: each waiver carries the new expiry and a line saying why the upgrade waits.
