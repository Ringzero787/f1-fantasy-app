# F-062 — UC: Race winners and per-race league leaderboard

Created: 2026-09-18 · Status: building

## Goal
Undercut (root app). Free for every league. Today the standings only show season totals, so a league that fell behind has nothing to play for each weekend and `raceWins` is always 0. Recording a winner per race gives every league a weekly result, and gives League Pro (head-to-head, recap, awards) the data it needs.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] At scoring time the server writes, per league and race, the ranked member points for that race (`leagues/{id}/raceResults/{raceId}`) and increments `raceWins` on the winning member; ties credit every tied member.
- [ ] Re-running scoring for the same race is idempotent: no double increment (unit-tested with a repeated run).
- [ ] A backfill op (`aidlc op`, dry-run first) populates 2026 races where the data allows, and documents which races could not be reconstructed. Finding (2026-09-19 dry run): rosters allow none. Past Aces were overwritten, departed picks fold into `lockedPoints`, and a roster-based estimate gave 0 points for rounds 1 to 15 in every league. The backfill therefore writes only the latest scored race, from the server-written `members.lastRacePoints` (race-day points, without the qualifying and sprint points banked earlier that weekend), marked `estimated: true` with `basis: 'race_day_points'`. Partial results never count towards `raceWins`. Every other completed 2026 race is reported as not reconstructable.
- [ ] The server records which races have a leaderboard in `leagues/{id}.raceResultIds`, and the selector lists only those.
- [ ] League screen has a per-race selector showing that race's leaderboard, and the season table shows race wins.
- [ ] Clients cannot write `raceResults` or `raceWins` (rules test).

## Targets
- firebase (functions, rules)
- android
- ios

## Data
Derived league results only; no new personal data.

## Performance budget
Two extra document writes per league per race at scoring (the result and the id on the league document) plus one read of the league's results and at most one write per member whose win count changed (usually the winner only) to sync race wins; one document read when a race is selected.

## Out of scope
- Head-to-head pairing (F-063).
- Push notification for race winner (follow-up).
