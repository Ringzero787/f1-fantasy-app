# F-175 — UC: Charter, Pit Wall for every player in one league

Created: 2026-11-24 · Status: planned (slice 1 built)

## Goal
A second product beside the Pit Wall Pass. **Charter**, `pitwall.charter.season`, $39.99, charters one league for one season: every approved member reads paid Pit Wall while they remain in that league, the league is League Pro until the charter ends, and chartered leagues rank above other public leagues in search, lifted by weekly Rank Ups.

The full build plan, with its closed product decisions, copy and order of work, is `docs/design/charter/BUILD.md`. Its last section, "Checked against the code (2026-11-24)", lists where the plan and the code disagree. The decision is ADR-004 (`.aidlc/decisions/ADR-004-charter.md`). This feature supersedes F-060 and F-061.

The work lands as five slices on `feat/F-175-charter` (BUILD.md, "Order of work"). Only the criteria slice 1 delivers are ticked.

### Slice 2: purchase paths
- [x] `pitwall.charter.season` in `src/config/products.ts` (name "Charter", $39.99, the plan's description) and `KNOWN_PRODUCTS`; a pass token cannot grant a charter and a charter token cannot grant a pass (the Play URL names the product and its echo is compared; Apple checks `expectedProductId`; the grant branch is chosen by that verified product).
- [x] `validatePurchase` (Play, Apple) and the Stripe checkout and webhook write `leagues/{id}.charter` (owner only, not already chartered for the season on sale, flag on), set `isPublic`, `requireApproval`, `pro`, `proUntil` on that league only, and grant `charterAccess` to the owner and every approved member. Idempotent on the purchase (`charter_grants/{ref}`: the purchase id for a store, `stripe:{payment_intent}` for Stripe). The checkout callable refuses while the flag is false. Amazon is refused for Charter.
- [x] Refund or dispute clears that league's charter and its members' access, and never calls `revokePass`: Stripe `charge.refunded` (full refunds only) and `charge.dispute.created` (looked up by PaymentIntent), and the daily store refund sweep for Play and legacy Apple receipts. A refund clears only the charter that purchase paid for, and a refunded purchase never charters again.
- [x] Before any `charterAccess` is written: the app's "you already have a pass" check reads `users/{uid}.pass` (`ownsPersonalPass` in `src/pitwall/pass.ts`), not the claim; the server checkout already checks `users.pass`. The Pit Wall site only shows the pass offer to a reader without access, so a Charter member is not refused there; it never refused on the claim.
- [x] The personal pass and a charter never shorten each other's League Pro: `revokePass` (and the nightly pass expiry) leave a chartered league Pro until its charter ends; `markOwnedLeaguesPro` keeps the later end (`ownerProPatch`).
- [x] Nightly `pw-expireLeagueCharters` clears charters whose season has ended (`clearCharter`, reason `expired`).
- [x] App plumbing, no UI: `purchaseCharter(leagueId)` (refused in demo mode and on the Amazon build), the league kept in persisted `pendingCharterLeagueId` so a purchase completing on a later launch still names it, and the purchase handler that sends it, finishes the transaction after the server answers, and says when a paid charter could not be applied.
- [x] Tests: `functions/test/charterPurchase.test.js` (decisions, Stripe params and webhook, grant and refund against a fake Firestore: no pass written, a second owned league not made Pro, members keep their pass, replay and refund-replay, expiry, `revokePass` on a chartered league), `__tests__/store/charterPurchase.test.ts`, and a rules test for `charter_grants`.

Decisions made in slice 2, where the plan left room:
- **A paid charter that cannot be applied** (not the owner, already chartered, no league id, flag off) is recorded, not thrown: on the purchase (`charterApplied: false`, `charterRefused`) and on `charter_grants`, logged as an error, and the device is told so it finishes the transaction and tells the buyer to contact support. Throwing would leave a paid receipt looping on the device; the record is what support refunds from.
- **Sandbox testing while the flag is off:** a Play license-tester purchase (`purchaseType 0`) or an Apple Sandbox transaction by an account marked `testAccount` (F-164, server-owned), and a Stripe checkout on an `sk_test_` key, may charter while `config/app.charter.enabled` is false, so slice 5 can test without opening the sale. A store test purchase by any other account never charters, with the sale on or off (the App Review accounts are marked). Any other purchase needs the flag. Test charters are recorded (`charterTest` on the purchase); the flag-flip op clears them first.
- **One purchase, one league:** once a purchase has chartered a league, a replay naming another league is answered with the first (`charter_grants/{ref}.leagueId` wins, inside the grant transaction); the league first recorded on the purchase wins over one sent later. Proven against the emulator with parallel replays (`rules-tests/charter.integration.test.js`).
- **A league over 22 approved players (grown by the retired slot pack) is refused** (`too-large`) at checkout and at grant: one charter is never more than 22 players' access.
- **Refunds are recorded even when nothing is live:** a refund or dispute that arrives before its grant, or for a purchase that was never applied, writes `charter_grants/{ref}.clearedAt`, so a later grant or replay of it is refused. `validatePurchase` also refuses a purchase the sweep marked refunded.
- **Clearing is retryable:** a retry for the same purchase finishes the member-access sweep even after the league was cleared, unless the league has been chartered again by another purchase.
- **Stripe:** the webhook trusts the flag check made at checkout (the buyer has paid by then) and checks the price instead: `amount_subtotal` 3999 in `usd`. Partial refunds are ignored for the charter and now for the pass too (a pass refund used to arrive without a uid, so none ever revoked).
- **Clearing a charter turns `isPublic` and `requireApproval` off**, as BUILD.md says; the owner's settings from before the charter are not restored.
- A charter never shortens League Pro the owner's own pass already gives (`proUntil` is the later of the two).
- **Renewal:** a live charter for the season before does not block buying the next season (from December, as the pass); the new charter replaces it with the later expiry.
- **Stripe metadata** is now also set on the PaymentIntent (`payment_intent_data[metadata]`), because a refund event carries only the charge's metadata. This also gives the personal pass's Stripe refunds the uid they never had (they were ignored as "refund without a uid").
- A League Pro trial is refused in a chartered league: its members already read Pit Wall.

## Data
- `leagues/{id}.charter = { season, expiresAt, buyerId, source: 'stripe'|'play'|'apple', ref, purchasedAt }`, `chartered`, `searchPoints`, `rankUpsWeek = { weekId, count }`: server-written only.
- `leagues/{id}/rankUps/{weekId}_{fromUid}`: server-written only.
- `users/{uid}.charterAccess = { leagues: { [leagueId]: expiresAt } }`: server-written only. It holds league ids and dates, no new personal data.
- The `pw` custom claim: unchanged shape, now the later of the pass and charter access.
- `config/app.charter.enabled`: admin-only write (existing `config` rule), default absent = off.
