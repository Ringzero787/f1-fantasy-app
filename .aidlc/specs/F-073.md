# F-073 — UC: Pit Wall lineup lab

Created: 2026-09-19 · Status: building

## Goal
Undercut (root app). The page that turns insight into action: the user sees their real lineup, what the portal would change and why, tries alternatives, and saves the change without picking up their phone.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Free: view the lineup, edit it manually and save, and see the **Rate My Team** score (0 to 100 against the best lineup for the user's budget) with the single biggest weakness named. Pass: ranked recommendations, top pick, what-if projections, optimizer, transfer planner and hindsight.
- [ ] Shows the signed-in user's team(s) exactly as the app does: five drivers, constructor, ace, budget, bank, contracts remaining, lock state. Team switcher for users with more than one team.
- [ ] Recommendations: ranked single and double swaps with projected gain (median and range), cost, fee impact, price-change effect and a one-line reason; an ace recommendation; a "rate my team" score against the optimal lineup for the user's budget.
- [ ] Tapping a lineup tile shows a **top pick** to replace that driver or constructor: the highest projected gain affordable within the bank (ace doubling included), as a side-by-side comparison with a reason and a one-tap swap; when nothing affordable projects higher it says so and shows the best alternative. Under it, a table of all affordable options with their stats (gain, price, projection, points per $, circuit fit, DNF risk, next price), current pick pinned as the first row, tap a row to swap.
- [ ] What-if: drag or tap to swap any asset; totals, budget, fees, projected points and league win probability update instantly; invalid lineups explain why (budget, contract, lock).
- [ ] Optimizer: locks, excludes, budget and risk preference (floor / median / ceiling); ranks lineups by expected league finish using ownership in the user's league, not only by mean points.
- [ ] Transfer planner: next five rounds with budget trajectory and circuit-fit colouring.
- [ ] Hindsight: optimal lineup for each past round and the user's gap to it; decision quality by category (ace, transfers, constructor); projection-at-lock vs actual.
- [ ] **Save lineup** calls the same server callables the app uses (server-authoritative rosters, budget and fees); the web never writes team documents directly. After lock the control is disabled with the lock status shown. A rules test proves a web client cannot bypass the callables.
- [ ] A change saved on the web appears in the app on next open, and the reverse.

## Targets
- web
- firebase (functions, rules)

## Data
Reads and updates the user's own teams through existing callables.

## Performance budget
What-if recalculation < 50 ms client-side; optimizer result < 2 s.

## Out of scope
- Auto-applying recommendations without the user pressing save.
- Creating teams or joining leagues on the web.

## Build notes (2026-09-24)
Built: the real roster in the Lineup Lab, a pure `planSave` that turns a what-if into the callable sequence the app's Pick Team uses, the pre-save summary of sales, fees and purchases, the team switcher for users with more than one team, and the Ace change.

- Fees quoted before saving mirror the server's `quoteSale` exactly, and the server stays authoritative: it is re-read immediately before the save and it stops at the first step it refuses.
- The Ace is the one field written directly. The rules let an owner set `aceDriverId` and deny every field carrying money or points, and scoring re-checks the price cap when it counts the weekend, so a forged Ace earns nothing. There is no `setAceSecure` callable to route through.
- Recommendations, the top pick and what-if are pass features (F-068); manual editing, saving and Rate My Team are free.

Not built here: the optimizer, the transfer planner and hindsight against real history (they need the projection payloads from F-070 and the snapshots from F-029), and double swaps.
