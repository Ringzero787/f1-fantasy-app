# F-115 — UC: Pit Wall tables read larger on a wide screen

Created: 2026-10-08 · Merged 2026-10-08 (PR #198) · Status: implemented · Design source: the owner, 2026-10-08 — "the font size on something like /market can be larger for driver names; we want it reactive for mobile, which is why we made it smaller, but on a PC we can scale it up"

## Goal
Undercut (Pit Wall portal). The portal's running text is 12 px mono at every width, and every table cell inherits it — the size was chosen so the phone layout holds, and nothing steps it up where there is room. On a desktop the Market, Board, Lineup Lab option and League tables are harder to read than they need to be, driver names most of all. This scales table text up at the portal's existing wide breakpoint and leaves the phone exactly as it is.

## Acceptance criteria
- [x] **At ≥ 981 px** (the breakpoint the page grid already uses) every `table` in the portal reads at 13 px and column headers at 11 px — cell padding stays as it is, because wider cells pushed the half-width tables (Lineup Lab options, Season) into sideways scroll at 1024–1280 px — Market, Board, Lineup Lab options, Season, Pace Lab, the compare table, the League tile. The bold name cell reads at 15 px: the first cell of a row everywhere, and the second in the League tile, where rank comes first (`#league-table`). Bold cells that are not names (a Board median, a Lab gain) are untouched. Below 981 px nothing changes: the phone keeps 12 px and its padding (byte-identical screenshot).
- [x] **Row lists keep their size.** `.row` lists (Circuit, Briefing, Moonshot) are not in scope; only `table` cells.
- [x] **Budgets hold.** The scroll budget stays within 3.0 screens in every measured state at 1440 × 900 (the larger cells lengthen the Board and Market pages); the half-width tables add no sideways scroll at 1024, 1280 or 1440 that they did not have before; first-load JS unchanged. Before/after screenshots of Market at desktop and phone in `docs/design/pitwall/verify/F-115-*.png`.
