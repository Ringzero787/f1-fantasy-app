# F-043 — TL: stake-scaled win bonus

Created: 2026-09-17 · Status: planned

## Goal

A correct call pays a flat $10 today whether or not the player put money on it, so the reward tracks how many picks someone taps rather than how much conviction they showed. Since the flat bonus went live (2026-07-26, four races) 71 of the 83 graded calls carried no stake at all, and staked amounts have shrunk: winning calls in the pre-bonus era included $50 and $100 stakes, while the post-bonus era tops out at $25. This adds a conviction top-up — a correct call that had money on it earns 50% of the stake on top of the flat $10, capped at $25 extra. Free calls are untouched. The point is to move money that already exists between players instead of minting more: the garage pool holds $17,482 across 173 players, but 128 of those sit on an untouched $100 opening balance and only $150 has ever been spent in the store, so a flat bonus increase would inflate a currency that already has no demand.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A winning call with no stake pays exactly WIN_BONUS ($10), unchanged from today.
- [ ] A winning call with stake S pays stake × odds + $10 + min($25, 0.5 × S).
- [ ] The stake bonus is capped: any stake of $50 or more earns exactly $25 extra and no more.
- [ ] A missing, zero or negative stake never pays more than the flat WIN_BONUS.
- [ ] The stake bonus is never multiplied by BEST_BET_PROFIT_MULT — an AGAINST best-bet win boosts only the profit on the stake.
- [ ] A losing call is unaffected: payout 0 and penalty LOSS_PENALTY, whatever the stake was.
- [ ] STAKE_BONUS_RATE and STAKE_BONUS_MAX are exported and asserted in newgame/functions/test/settlement.test.js.
- [ ] The in-app explainer on the prediction pill states the stake top-up and its cap.

## Targets
- android
- ios

## Data
No user data. Changes only the cash figures written during settlement: `tl_garages/{uid}.cash` and the `payout` inside `tl_picks/{doc}.settledOutcomes`.

## Performance budget
n/a — one multiplication and one clamp per graded call.

## Out of scope
- Changing LOSS_PENALTY, or making the penalty stake-scaled to match.
- Re-settling past weekends at the new rate. A re-grade re-prices through `garageApplied`, so this deploys between weekends, never mid-settlement.
- Cosmetics pack pricing and the store sink — the larger reason garage cash has nowhere to go.
- Streak bonuses (the `streak_bonus` TransactionType already exists but is unused here).
