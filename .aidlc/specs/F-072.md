# F-072 — UC: Pit Wall driver, constructor and circuit reports

Created: 2026-09-19 · Status: planned

## Goal
Undercut (root app). The research pages, modelled on the frames sports analytics and betting-research tools use, translated to this sport and to Undercut scoring.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Briefing:** what-changed cards with impact chips, lock countdown, the user's lineup health, then a clickable **Recommendations** list placed above top moves and price movers. Each recommendation (swap, ace, hold, risk, constructor) opens a side-by-side comparison of the user's current pick against the recommended or nearest-priced alternative across the data on the page (projection range, floor, ceiling, points per $, circuit fit, next price, DNF risk, league ownership, price), marks the better value per row, gives a one-line reason, and offers "try this swap" which carries the change into the Lineup Lab as a what-if. Hold and keep recommendations are shown too, so the user sees why a pick is right. Below it: **rivals' likely moves** (for each close rival in the user's league: their best affordable swap from their bank and lineup, a likelihood weighted by how often that manager edits, and a tag: THREAT when they would gain a driver the user does not own, COPIES YOU when they would erase one of the user's differentials, LOW RISK for managers who rarely edit; click opens the incoming driver), price movers, weather by session. Rival data is limited to leagues the user belongs to and to what the app already shows league members.
- [ ] **Board:** sortable projection table (floor / median / ceiling range bar, price, value, plus/minus, consistency, DNF risk, ownership, form sparkline, next-price arrow) with column-set presets Value / Pace / Ownership / Risk; model probability matrix (win, podium, top 10, DNF); projection-movement chart across the weekend.
- [ ] **Entity slide-over** (driver or constructor, opened from anywhere): Past / Present / Outlook tabs; hit-rate strip with adjustable threshold and L5/L10/Season; splits (street, high-speed, high-downforce, wet, sprint); seven-axis season profile (qualifying, starts, lap one, race pace, tyre management, pit stops, overtaking); teammate head-to-head split bars; price history; tagged news; Outlook text from F-071.
- [ ] **Circuit:** circuit profile (straight-line share, corner-speed mix, overtaking difficulty, pit loss, safety-car rate), car circuit-fit ranking, history splits at this venue, weather and each driver's wet/dry delta, typical strategies.
- [ ] **Pace Lab:** long-run pace and degradation by compound from practice, lap-time heat map (driver x lap) as an index into stints, quali vs race pace quadrant scatter, sector-rank matrix, pit stop medians by team.
- [ ] Every frame follows the F-075 scroll budget and click-in rules, has a table view, and shows "data as of".
- [ ] Verified with Playwright screenshots of each page in dark and light at desktop and phone widths.
