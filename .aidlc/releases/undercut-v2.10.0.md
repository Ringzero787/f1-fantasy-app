# uc-v2.10.0 — 2026-11-25

Undercut 2.10.0: find a public league, join requests, faster lineup saves

## Added
- **Find a public league**: browse public leagues and ask to join with one tap.
- **Join requests**: league owners see requests in one place and approve or decline each player.
- **League slot packs**: add 3 player slots at a time, once your store lists them.

## Changed

- **Faster lineup saves**: your lineup now saves in one step.
- The +20 slot pack is no longer sold; packs already bought still apply.

## Behind the scenes

- Charter (Pit Wall for a whole league) is built but not on sale yet.
