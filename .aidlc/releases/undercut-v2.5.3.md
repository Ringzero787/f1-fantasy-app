# uc-v2.5.3 — 2026-10-12

Undercut 2.5.3: the league round report, Pit Wall's ace call, and a tidier sign-in.

## Added

- Round report: after each race, see how your league did on one screen: the podium, the biggest climb and drop, the best value per dollar, and every Moonshot that landed or missed. Open it from the League tab, or after your weekend recap.
- With a Pit Wall Pass, Pit Wall now says which driver to ace, on the Team screen and in each driver's sheet.

## Changed

- Sign-in buttons show each provider's own mark and wording.

## Fixed

- The driver sheet scrolls on iOS.
- Every screen reads properly at the largest display size.
- The team name is saved once when return is pressed, not twice.
